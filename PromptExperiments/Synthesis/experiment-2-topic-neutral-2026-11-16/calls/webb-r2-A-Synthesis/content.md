### Central insight
Webb’s fully deployed configuration after two weeks was not the same as readiness for routine science: cooling, mirror alignment and instrument calibration continued through a six-month commissioning period.

### Evidence
1. Webb was too large for the available Ariane 5 fairing in its operational configuration, so it was designed to fold for launch and deploy in flight.
2. Deployment involved more than 50 major steps, 178 release mechanisms and over 300 possible single points of failure; the source says any one could have diminished or crippled the mission, and repair was not possible at roughly one million miles from Earth. Remaining deployments were choreographed from ground control and monitored through telemetry.
3. In the second week, the secondary mirror and both primary-mirror wings unfolded and latched, completing Webb’s fully deployed operational configuration.
4. During the first month, the telescope and instruments cooled for several weeks. Heaters controlled the cooling so components would shrink carefully and trapped water could escape as gas rather than freeze on mirrors or detectors.
5. Mirror alignment took place in months two through four; instrument modes were calibrated in months five and six. Webb began routine science operations after six months. Early images showed distorted views of the same star while the primary mirror segments were not yet aligned as one mirror.

### What the narrative should emphasize
Keep the distinction between deployment and readiness for routine science at the center. The launch constraint and deployment risks establish the stakes; the later cooling, alignment and calibration milestones show that reaching the deployed configuration was only one part of the process. The distorted early star images are an optional concrete detail, not a required detour. Explain Webb’s documented commissioning process, not a general prescription for engineering projects.

### What should be de-emphasized
Do not turn the account into a step-by-step inventory of every deployment mechanism or a full mission timeline. Details such as the radiators, final orbit maneuver and Early Release Observations are not needed to establish the distinction between unfolding and readiness for routine science.

### Gaps and uncertainty
The source reports that deployment proceeded smoothly but does not identify any anomaly or provide step-by-step telemetry results. Do not imply that a specific deployment problem occurred or was resolved. The source also does not give exact calibration thresholds or the exact date routine science operations began.
