### Central insight
Webb’s deployment completed its physical unfolding in the second week, but that did not make it ready for routine science: cooling, mirror alignment, and instrument calibration continued through the following months, and routine science began after six months.

### Evidence
1. Deployment involved more than 50 major steps and over 300 possible single points of failure; the source says any one could have diminished or crippled the mission, and repair was not possible at roughly one million miles from Earth. The remaining deployments were choreographed from ground control and monitored through telemetry.
2. In the second week, the secondary mirror and both primary-mirror wings unfolded and latched, completing Webb’s fully deployed operational configuration.
3. After deployment, the telescope and instruments cooled for several weeks; the team aligned the mirrors during months two through four and calibrated instrument modes during months five and six. Webb began routine science operations after six months.

### What the narrative should emphasize
Keep the distinction between completing deployment and becoming ready for routine science at the center. The key evidence is the second-week completion of the physical configuration and the subsequent months of cooling, alignment, and calibration before routine operations. Explain the documented sequence, not as advice to readers or proof that one approach is universally superior. The source characterizes deployment as remarkably smooth, so the potential risks should not be presented as failures that actually occurred.

### What should be de-emphasized
The exact sequence of individual sunshield and mirror mechanisms, detailed cooling controls, orbit maneuvers, and the full list of deployment hardware are not needed unless one detail helps clarify the distinction between unfolding and commissioning. The launch dimensions and fairing fit are useful context for the compact-launch premise, but are not necessary to establish the central insight.

### Gaps and uncertainty
The source does not identify a specific deployment anomaly or explain whether any issue was encountered and resolved. It reports a remarkably smooth deployment, but does not provide detailed telemetry results. Do not imply that the listed risks materialized, or specify unreported alignment or calibration thresholds.
