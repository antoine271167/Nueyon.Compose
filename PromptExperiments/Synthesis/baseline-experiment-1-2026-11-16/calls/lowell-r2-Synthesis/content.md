### Central insight
Lowell tried to repurpose its industrial heritage as a basis for preservation and new activity, but the revival described in the 1992 account was not a straightforward recovery: the boom had ended by the late 1980s.

### Evidence
1. After postwar orders fell, the Boott and Merrimack mills closed in the 1950s and mill employment nearly disappeared. (Fact 6)
2. In the 1960s, Lowell citizens proposed revitalization based on the city’s architectural and cultural heritage, including a historical park; political and business leaders, groups, and government agencies supported and cooperated with the effort. (Facts 7–8)
3. The source reports rising employment and business expansion in the late 1970s and early 1980s, alongside more than 100 old buildings rehabilitated for new uses. It does not attribute these developments solely to the parks. (Fact 10)
4. By the late 1980s, the boom had ended: the regional economy cooled, companies closed or relocated, and Wang cut most of its workforce and restructured. (Fact 11)

### What the narrative should emphasize
Keep the story on the contrast between Lowell’s heritage-based revitalization effort and the later limits of its boom. The reported building reuse is a concrete illustration, not proof that preservation alone caused the wider recovery. Treat this as an account of Lowell’s own history, not as a prescription for how other cities should redevelop.

### What should be de-emphasized
The detailed chronology of textile decline and the separate roles of the University of Lowell and Wang’s arrival are not needed beyond the context in Evidence 1. Avoid turning those developments into a complete account of Lowell’s economy or assigning any one institution sole credit for the revival.

### Gaps and uncertainty
The account does not quantify the separate contributions of parks, preservation, the university, Wang, or other factors to employment or recovery. It also does not establish whether the revival offset earlier losses for affected workers. Its descriptions are from 1992 and do not establish conditions after that account.
