26. The source states that the source-defined responsibility of the Narrative stage is to establish the central story and its progression.
27. The source states that the source-defined responsibility of the Compose stage is to transform the narrative into finished content in the requested format.

29. The source states that Compose is not simply intended to be another text generator and that it is an attempt to build a coordinated process for turning ideas and knowledge into publishable content.
