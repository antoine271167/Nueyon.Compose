
Nuëyon.Compose as a concrete expression of an orchestration-first approach to making AI useful
Central insight: Nuëyon.Compose demonstrates the company’s core principle that useful AI products are built as coordinated, staged workflows of specialized capabilities (or agents) rather than as isolated single-prompt LLM interactions. The six-stage content workflow (Idea → Discover and Select → Research → Synthesize → Narrative → Compose) is presented as the concrete implementation of that orchestration principle.
