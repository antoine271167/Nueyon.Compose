# Evaluation rubric

Each dimension is scored 0–2 for each run. Dimensions are independent; run gate requires **2 in every dimension** and no material factual error.

## 1. Content integrity
- **2:** Claims, facts, audience, actors, motives, relationships, outcomes and certainty stay within source/Synthesis; no material error.
- **1:** Minor ambiguity or weakly supported phrasing, no material factual error.
- **0:** Material invention, scope change, audience expansion, unsupported motive/result/cause, or certainty inflation.

## 2. Editorial direction
- **2:** Preserves selected story and central message; for the company introduction Nuëyon remains primary, Compose is the first concrete product direction, orchestration supports the story.
- **1:** Mostly aligned but hierarchy is diluted or technical framing competes.
- **0:** Central message or hierarchy materially changes.

## 3. Editorial quality
- **2:** Specific opening with supported pull; coherent progression where steps add distinct information; clear, readable, strong natural phrasing that holds attention without format conventions.
- **1:** Understandable but generic, list-like, repetitive, weakly paced, or only mildly engaging.
- **0:** Flat/confusing/incoherent, hard to read, or no clear central thread.

## Non-compensation and comparison
A run passes only at 2/2/2 with no material factual error. Candidate must pass 3/3 runs and must not score below its paired baseline in any dimension; candidate must show editorial-quality improvement without regressions. Material failures are hard failures. Report selected Idea, Research, Synthesis, Narrative, and Compose evidence and earliest observable drift. Distinguish input divergence from Narrative output change and Compose-only changes.
