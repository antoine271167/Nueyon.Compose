### Facts

1. The library calls its bilingual reading cards “a visible expression of our commitment to language access.”
2. The source states that this is the institution’s stated meaning of the cards, not a measured user response.
3. The pilot supplied 24 printed cards in Spanish and Arabic at two branches for four weeks.
4. Staff received 18 unsolicited comments.
5. Of those comments, 13 were favorable.
6. Five comments reported unclear wording.
7. Visitors were not sampled systematically.
8. The number of visitors who used the cards is unknown.
9. Staff found incorrect opening hours.
10. Three Arabic cards were revised after staff found incorrect opening hours.
11. The Spanish cards had correct opening hours.
12. Two Spanish readers reported wording difficulty.
13. One Arabic reader praised the welcoming design.
14. That Arabic reader reported a wrong closing time.
15. No comprehension or belonging survey was conducted.
16. A website version is proposed.
17. The proposed website version has no release date.
18. The website version has not been implemented.
19. The annual circulation total predates the pilot.
20. The annual circulation total cannot measure the pilot’s effect.

### Unknowns

- Whether comprehension improved is not established. The source reports favorable comments but no comprehension survey; those comments do not establish improved comprehension.
- Whether belonging improved is not established. The source reports favorable comments but no belonging survey; those comments do not establish improved belonging.
- The source does not establish what proportion of visitors used the cards; it says the number of users is unknown and visitors were not sampled systematically.
- The source does not establish whether the 18 staff-received comments represent visitors’ responses generally.
- The source does not state why the three Arabic cards were revised; it establishes that the revisions followed staff finding incorrect opening hours.
- The source does not establish why an Arabic reader reported a wrong closing time while the Spanish cards had correct opening hours.
- The source does not establish whether the proposed website version will be released or when.
- The annual circulation total cannot establish the pilot’s effect, according to the source.
