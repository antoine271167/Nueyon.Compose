### Facts

1. In April, the East reservoir recorded 14 pressure alerts in 20 operating days under the earlier valve schedule.
2. During May, the East reservoir used a new valve schedule.
3. During May, the East reservoir recorded 8 pressure alerts in 20 operating days.
4. May industrial demand was 30% lower.
5. The May alert threshold was 1.8 bar.
6. The earlier alert threshold was 2.0 bar.
7. The note does not report how many April alerts fell between 1.8 and 2.0 bar.
8. The West reservoir retained the earlier valve schedule in both months.
9. The West reservoir used the 2.0 bar threshold in both months.
10. West reservoir alerts fell from 12 to 7.
11. Demand at the West reservoir fell 28%.
12. The West reservoir is supplied by a separate pump.
13. The note describes West as contextual contrary evidence, not a randomized control.
14. The operations team described the May East schedule as a promising operational trial, not an established improvement.
15. No randomized assignment was performed.
16. No threshold-adjusted reanalysis was performed.
17. Maintenance costs were not measured.
18. Water quality was not measured.
19. A new remote sensor is planned for July.
20. The new remote sensor was not installed during either month.

### Development sequence

- April: East recorded 14 alerts in 20 operating days under the earlier schedule (Fact 1).
- May: East used a new schedule and recorded 8 alerts in 20 operating days (Facts 2–3). The relationship between the schedule and the alert-count change is UNKNOWN.
- Across May and April: May had 30% lower industrial demand, and the alert threshold differed (Facts 4–6). Their effects on the East alert-count change are UNKNOWN.
- Across the two months: West retained the earlier schedule and 2.0 bar threshold (Facts 8–9); its alerts fell from 12 to 7 while demand fell 28% (Facts 10–11). The relationship between the West alert change and demand change is UNKNOWN. The note describes West as contextual contrary evidence, not a randomized control (Fact 13).
- The operations team characterized the May East schedule as a promising operational trial, not an established improvement (Fact 14). No randomized assignment or threshold-adjusted reanalysis was performed (Facts 15–16).
- A remote sensor is planned for July and was not installed in either reported month (Facts 19–20).
