# Experiment 2: source-first Research judge calibration

Frozen before any live call, 2026-11-12. Hard maximum: 12 live gpt-6-luna HTTP requests, zero SDK retries, no repeated attempts. Every attempt consumes budget even on transport/parser failure. No Research generation, repair, Synthesis model, retrieval or other model calls. No Copilot CLI, commit or push.

One generic candidate is frozen before evaluating anything. Calls 1–3: unchanged frozen intro/005/010 baselines. Calls 4–6: unchanged previous targeted controls. Call 7: strengthened 005 control additionally covering the previously diagnosed Synthesis tension and narrowing the blanket Unknown about lessons. Calls 8–12: independently authored synthetic holdouts in unrelated domains (two correct, three defective). Holdouts are independent of historical project cases, but are authored/labeled by the same unblinded evaluator; not an independent human validation. No prompt adjustment after seeing outcomes and no reuse of leftover calls for tuning.

Procedure: source-first inventory of material propositions, explicit meanings, causal boundaries and counterexamples for every Selected Idea claim; compare with Research Facts/Unknowns and actual production-filter output; clause-level Unknown negation check; emit only source-anchored material diagnoses. Internal inventory is not added to the public JSON contract. Exact sourceQuote and contract are checked by the unchanged production parser.

Gate: all three historical target defects explicitly diagnosed, not merely rejected for another issue; no false rejection of correct information in controls; diagnoses must be materially relevant, supported by exact original-source evidence and free of invented premises. Old 005 control can fail for its remaining genuine omission, but must not reject preserved correct facts or repeat its repaired upstream-variation issue. Strengthened 005 and other correct controls should pass. Holdouts: both correct accepted and all three defects detected faithfully, with no serious regression. All gates required for promotion. On failure, do not change any production prompt and leave EnableResearchJudge disabled. On pass only the ResearchValidation system prompt may be promoted; activation and workflow changes are outside this experiment.

Persist request, raw response, HTTP metadata, attempt marker, parsed verdict or error, input, Research, actual filter output, usage, candidate hash, labels and human assessment. Report tuningset and holdouts separately. Offline build and full tests required. Compare protected generator/filter/workflow hashes and verify current default/configuration. This small single-shot test cannot establish repeatability, population accuracy or downstream quality.
