Nuëyon’s introduction was meant to lead with its AI-first ambition for other AI-first businesses. Nuëyon.Compose was the first concrete product direction; orchestration was supporting context, not the main message.

A candidate Narrative prompt added a general instruction to preserve the primary message, audience, and emphasis established in the synthesis. Across all three candidate runs, orchestration instead became central, and the company-level message was displaced or diluted. The candidate’s mean scores were also lower than baseline at both the Narrative and Compose stages.

The candidate was not promoted, and the production Narrative prompt was restored byte-for-byte. In these runs, the generic instruction did not preserve the intended hierarchy.
