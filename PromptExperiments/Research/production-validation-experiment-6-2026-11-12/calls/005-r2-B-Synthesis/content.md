### Central insight
In this three-run comparison, the generic Narrative instruction did not preserve Nuëyon’s intended message hierarchy: every candidate run made orchestration central, and the candidate was not promoted.

### Evidence
1. The experiment’s intended hierarchy put Nuëyon’s AI-first ambition for other AI-first businesses first, Compose as its first concrete product direction, and orchestration in a supporting role. The candidate added a generic instruction to preserve Synthesis’s message and emphasis; only the Narrative user prompt changed.
2. All three candidate Narrative outputs and all three candidate Compose outputs made orchestration central and failed the factual/editorial-restraint criterion. Candidate mean rubric scores were lower than baseline in both stages: Narrative, 4.00 versus 6.00; Compose, 3.33 versus 5.67.
3. The candidate was not promoted, and the production Narrative prompt was restored byte-for-byte to baseline.

### What the narrative should emphasize
Keep the story about the result of this specific prompt test: the generic instruction did not preserve the intended hierarchy in the observed candidate runs, and the team chose not to promote it. Describe Nuëyon’s intended hierarchy as the test’s target, not as a universal rule for editorial workflows. Do not imply the prompt change alone explains the failures beyond what these runs establish.

### What should be de-emphasized
Do not broaden this into a general product or workflow account, or turn the result into advice that generic hierarchy instructions will fail generally. The candidate’s readability and Compose-placement results are secondary to the hierarchy and restraint findings.

### Gaps and uncertainty
There were only three runs per condition; the rubric scores are judgments of saved outputs, so the results do not establish statistical significance or general performance across settings. The report also does not isolate whether the baseline Synthesis’s mixed signals about orchestration, or a possible change in the provider backend behind the model alias, contributed to the observed difference.
