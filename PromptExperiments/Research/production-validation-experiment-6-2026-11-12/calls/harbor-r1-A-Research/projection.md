### Facts

1. The dossier describes HarborLink as a fictional regional operator and the material as a synthetic operational source, not a real transport study.
2. The demonstration involved six leased electric buses at Seabank depot and ran for eight weeks.
3. The operator described the buses as “a visible first step toward cleaner local transport.”
4. The dossier says that phrase expresses the operator’s intended meaning, not a measured finding about emissions, public trust, or service quality.
5. The board’s decision boundary was whether to commission a winter assessment, not whether to replace the entire fleet.
6. No procurement approval had been given.
7. The comparison period ran from 9 March to 3 May, and the demonstration ran from 4 May to 28 June.
8. Both periods contained eight weeks.
9. The school holiday calendar differed between the periods.
10. The comparison period contained two weeks of bridge diversion; the demonstration period contained none.
11. Dispatchers assigned the electric buses to two relatively flat routes close to the depot and retained diesel buses for a longer hill route.
12. The source says the figures are not normalized fleet-effect estimates.
13. In the comparison period, the six vehicles later displaced by the trial completed 2,240 of 2,400 scheduled departures, a 93.3% completion rate.
14. During the trial, the electric group completed 2,310 of 2,400 scheduled departures, a 96.3% completion rate.
15. The final dispatch ledger is the source of the departure counts.
16. A draft presentation used 2,340 trial departures; 30 entries were duplicate departure IDs and were removed on 9 July.
17. The source says the draft figure must not be used for the completion comparison.
18. Completion rate used the same definition in both periods.
19. In March–April, on-time arrival meant no more than three minutes late; in May–June, it meant no more than five minutes late.
20. The dashboard displays on-time arrival rates of 82% and 89%, respectively.
21. The old GPS records were not reprocessed under a common threshold.
22. The source says the higher displayed punctuality percentage combines possible service differences with a measurement change.
23. The source says completion rate does not have this particular threshold problem and that completion and punctuality must remain distinct measures.
24. The diesel vehicles on the hill route completed 1,170 of 1,200 departures before the demonstration and 1,188 of 1,200 during it.
25. Those diesel vehicles used neither electric propulsion nor the new charger schedule.
26. Roadworks ended on part of the hill route in April.
27. The source describes the hill-route figures as a contextual comparison, not a randomized control.
28. The source says the different terrain and dispatch rules prevent a clean effect estimate, and that the comparison prevents treating every contemporaneous improvement as unique to the electric group.
29. The electric buses used 42,000 kWh measured at the depot chargers; the figure includes charging losses up to the vehicle connection and excludes depot offices.
30. The electric buses’ recorded distance was 35,000 km.
31. The energy team reports 1.20 kWh/km at the depot-charger meter boundary.
32. A brochure quoted 1.05 kWh/km from vehicle telemetry, a different boundary.
33. Neither energy figure is a certified whole-life emissions estimate.
34. No manufacturing, battery replacement, or grid-displacement analysis was commissioned.
35. Electricity expenditure was EUR 8,400 under a temporary demonstration tariff.
36. The six comparable diesel vehicles previously used EUR 13,700 of fuel while driving 38,000 km.
37. The source says the diesel and electricity expenditures involve different distances, traffic conditions, and energy sources.
38. Charger lease, electrical upgrade, standing charges, and workshop training are not included in the electricity figure.
39. The temporary tariff expires on 31 October.
40. Finance had not produced a like-for-like whole-cost calculation.
41. The source says the recorded expenditures may be quoted with their boundaries, but a proven annual saving or fleet payback period is not supported.
42. Two chargers were available.
43. During three overnight sessions, one charger was offline after a protective trip.
44. Two buses departed on time after using the other charger; a third was substituted with a diesel bus.
45. The final ledger counts the substituted trip as a completed route departure but not as an electric-bus departure.
46. The source describes the summary completion rate as a service-delivery measure, not a measure of exclusive electric operation.
47. The charger vendor replaced a connector and updated firmware in the same week.
48. The source says the record cannot identify which of those actions resolved the trips.
49. Staff handed feedback cards to riders on four Tuesday mornings; 148 cards were returned.
50. Of the returned cards, 112 described the ride as quieter, 21 were neutral, and 15 reported another issue without answering the noise question.
51. The total number of riders approached and the number of unique riders are unknown; the same regular rider could return cards in more than one week.
52. The source describes the cards as volunteered comments from selected sessions, not 148 independently sampled citizens or a representative satisfaction score.
53. Nine drivers participated in a debrief; seven described the cab as easier to converse in, and two reported unfamiliar braking response.
54. The driver observations were collected after a training session and without blinded comparison.
55. The source says those observations do not establish fewer collisions or better driver health.
56. The safety register recorded no collision in either eight-week period for these vehicles.
57. The source says the small exposure with zero events does not establish equal long-term collision risk and does not warrant claims about medical outcomes.
58. Accessibility reviewers inspected three buses; all three had the specified wheelchair space.
59. Two of the three buses had an audio-stop announcement failure during inspection; the third passed both physical-space and audio checks.
60. The source identifies physical access and functioning announcements as separate dimensions.
61. The maintenance team restored the two audio units on 2 July, after the trial ended.
62. The source says the final report must preserve both the trial-time failures and the later repair status, and must not present the whole demonstration as fully accessible merely because the space dimensions passed.
63. Demonstration daytime temperatures ranged from 14 to 25 degrees Celsius; there were no freezing days and no winter heating loads.
64. The leased buses had not been tested on the hill route in winter.
65. The engineering team proposed a winter run with heating and matched route allocation; the source says this proposal is not a completed trial and has no measured result.
66. A charging-capacity spreadsheet assumes each vehicle returns by 23:00; dispatch logs show five returns after that time.
67. The source describes the spreadsheet assumption as having observed exceptions, not as an established operating guarantee.
68. The maintenance register had 11 entries during the demonstration and 16 during the prior period.
69. The new form grouped repeated charger warnings into one incident, whereas the older form listed each warning.
70. The source says the count difference cannot by itself establish greater reliability.
71. There were two confirmed tire replacements in each period under the same parts coding.
72. The source says neither the tire entries nor the aggregate maintenance counts reveal an isolated propulsion effect.
73. On 18 September, the board authorized preparation of a winter-assessment specification within the existing planning budget.
74. The board did not authorize another bus lease, a depot conversion, or a procurement award.
75. The communications draft saying “the pilot has proved the business case for electrifying the fleet” was rejected.
76. The approved wording calls the demonstration “encouraging operational experience,” with unresolved cost, seasonality, and comparison questions.
77. The source characterizes that wording as an attributed board interpretation, not an independent causal finding.
78. The source says the public update should retain the concrete improvement in completed services without merging it with the incompatible punctuality series.
79. The source says the record supports a narrower next decision about evaluation design and does not support a general recommendation for every transport operator.

### Unknowns

- Whether the completion-rate difference between the comparison and demonstration periods was caused by electric propulsion; the source says the figures are not normalized fleet-effect estimates and describes assignment and route differences.
- A punctuality comparison under a common threshold; the old GPS records were not reprocessed under one.
- A certified whole-life emissions result or net-carbon reduction; the source says the available energy figures are not such an estimate and that relevant analyses were not commissioned.
- A like-for-like whole-cost calculation, proven annual saving, or fleet payback period.
- Which charger-vendor action, if either, resolved the trips during the sessions when one charger was offline.
- A representative rider satisfaction result or the number of unique riders and total riders approached.
- Whether the trial affected collision risk or driver health.
- Whether the buses would perform on the hill route in winter, including with heating loads; the proposed winter run has no measured result.
- Whether the charging-capacity assumption that vehicles return by 23:00 holds as an operating guarantee; five dispatch-log returns were later than that.
- The cause of the difference in aggregate maintenance-entry counts as a measure of reliability; the forms counted warnings differently.
- The reason the board authorized preparation of a winter-assessment specification, beyond the decision recorded in the minutes.
- A general recommendation for other transport operators; the source says the record does not support one.
