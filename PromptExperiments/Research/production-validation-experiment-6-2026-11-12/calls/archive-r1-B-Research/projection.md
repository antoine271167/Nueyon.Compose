### Facts

1. The dossier identifies City Memory as a fictional municipal archive and the report as synthetic, not a real archive report. Its ten-week pilot (6 April–14 June) created 900 catalogue records—300 each for selected shipping registers from Cedar, Elm and Rowan—not a catalogue of all holdings. (Sections 1, 7)
2. Here, digitization means a searchable catalogue entry plus a scanned page; it does not replace the paper original or establish verified full-text transcription. The source treats chronological order, accuracy of description and image availability as separate dimensions. (Section 1: “These dimensions require separate treatment”)
3. Reviewers checked 60 records per collection, selected from accessible boxes rather than through a random sample of all 300; fragile-binding boxes were excluded. The source says these observations are not full-collection error-rate estimates. (Section 2)
4. In the reviewed records, Cedar was correctly ordered in all 60, but 9 had a mistranscribed vessel name. Elm’s 60 vessel names matched the originals, but 8 entries were under the wrong year. Rowan passed both checks in all 60 reviewed records; the source says this does not establish that all 300 Rowan records are correct. (Section 2)
5. On 1 July, the dashboard rule changed from marking a collection green if either check passed to requiring both. On 8 July, staff corrected nine Cedar names and eight Elm dates and rechecked those 17 entries—not all 180 reviewed records or the other 720 catalogue records. The source characterizes the dashboard change as a reporting-rule correction, not an improvement to the underlying records. (Section 2)
6. Rights clearance is complete for 760 of the 900 scans. The remaining 140 descriptions are visible, but their page images are held internally and are not publicly downloadable. Review next quarter is an expectation, not a committed release date. (Section 5)
7. Paper originals remain in controlled storage and are available by appointment, subject to handling restrictions; the web catalogue has not replaced every route to the collection. (Sections 1, 6)
8. The committee approved designing a deeper random audit stratified by handwriting, print quality and collection. It has not approved full-archive rollout or a claim that assisted cataloguing outperforms manual work; the proposed audit has not been tested and has produced no new pass rate. (Section 7)

### Unknowns

- The source does not establish the ordering or description accuracy of all 900 records, or that the reviewed records represent the full collections; fragile-binding material was excluded and the review was not random. (Facts 3–5)
- It does not establish that all 900 page images are publicly available: 140 are withheld pending rights review, with no committed release date. (Fact 6)
- The proposed deeper audit has not yet been conducted, so no broader pass rate or full-archive quality conclusion is available. (Fact 8)
