### Facts

1. The pilot covered selected shipping registers from the Cedar, Elm and Rowan collections; it was not a catalogue of the archive’s full holdings.
2. The project ran for ten weeks, from 6 April to 14 June 2026.
3. The project created 900 catalogue records.
4. Each of the three collections contributed 300 records.
5. The paper originals remain in controlled storage.
6. In this project, “digitization” means a searchable catalogue entry plus a scanned page.
7. In this project, digitization does not mean that the original was replaced.
8. Full-text transcription was not verified.
9. The source states that images can be legible while their descriptive metadata is wrong.
10. The source states that a catalogue can have accurate names while putting records in the wrong order.
11. The source says these dimensions require separate treatment.
12. Reviewers checked 60 records per collection.
13. Staff selected the first 20 items in each of three accessible boxes rather than randomly sampling all 300 entries.
14. Boxes with fragile bindings were excluded.
15. The observations are about accessible reviewed records, not estimates of full-collection error rates.
16. For Cedar, all 60 reviewed records were in the correct chronological order.
17. For Cedar, 9 reviewed records had a mistranscribed vessel name.
18. For Elm, all 60 reviewed vessel names matched the originals.
19. For Elm, 8 reviewed entries were placed under the wrong year.
20. Rowan passed both checks in all 60 reviewed records.
21. The source states that Rowan’s reviewed results are not evidence that all 300 Rowan records are correct.
22. The committee’s first dashboard merged the checks into a single green flag whenever either check passed.
23. That first dashboard would have marked every collection green.
24. On 1 July, the dashboard was changed to require both checks.
25. The source describes that dashboard change as a reporting-rule correction, not an improvement to the underlying records.
26. Nine Cedar names and eight Elm dates were corrected on 8 July.
27. Reviewers rechecked those seventeen corrected entries.
28. Reviewers did not recheck all 180 reviewed entries or the remaining 720 catalogue records.
29. The limited recheck supports correction of those observed entries, not complete certification.
30. Rights clearance is complete for 760 of the 900 scans.
31. The remaining 140 scans are held internally pending review.
32. Catalogue descriptions for the 140 scans are visible.
33. The page images for the 140 scans are not publicly downloadable.
34. The rights team expects to review the remainder next quarter.
35. The source describes that review timing as a forecast without a committed release date.
36. The committee approved designing a deeper random audit stratified by handwriting, print quality and collection.
37. The committee has not approved a full-archive rollout.
38. The committee has not approved a claim that assisted cataloguing outperforms manual work.
39. The proposed audit would assess names, chronology, translation roles, publication rights and access separately.
40. The proposed audit design has not been tested, and no new pass rate exists.
41. For communication, the committee wants the pilot’s output acknowledged: 900 catalogue entries exist, and observed errors have received specific corrections.
42. The committee wants the distinct quality dimensions and publication boundary to remain visible.
43. The source states that the institution’s access commitment is explicit, while its effects on users are unmeasured.

### Unknowns

- The error rates across all records in the three collections, and across the archive’s full holdings, are not established by this review.
- The error profile of the excluded fragile-binding material is not established.
- Whether all catalogue records are currently accurate or correctly ordered is not established; the recheck covered only the seventeen corrected entries.
- The source does not establish that all 760 scans with completed rights clearance are publicly downloadable.
- The release date for the remaining 140 images is not established.
- A full-archive rollout has not been approved; whether one will be approved is not established.
- The committee’s approved audit design has not been tested, and its results are unknown.
- The effects of the access commitment on users are unmeasured.
