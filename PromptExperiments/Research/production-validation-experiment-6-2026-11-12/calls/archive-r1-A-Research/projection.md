### Facts

1. City Memory’s pilot covered selected shipping registers from the Cedar, Elm and Rowan collections; it was not a catalogue of the archive’s full holdings.
2. The pilot ran for ten weeks, from 6 April to 14 June 2026.
3. The pilot created 900 catalogue records, with 300 records from each collection.
4. Paper originals remain in controlled storage.
5. In this project, digitization means a searchable catalogue entry plus a scanned page; it does not mean the original was replaced or that full-text transcription was verified.
6. The source states that images can be legible while descriptive metadata is wrong.
7. The source states that a catalogue can have accurate names while records are in the wrong order.
8. The source states that these dimensions require separate treatment.
9. Reviewers checked 60 records per collection.
10. Staff selected the first 20 items in each of three accessible boxes rather than randomly sampling all 300 entries.
11. Boxes with fragile bindings were excluded from the review.
12. The review observations concern accessible reviewed records and are not estimates of full-collection error rates.
13. For Cedar, all 60 reviewed records were in the correct chronological order.
14. For Cedar, 9 of the 60 reviewed records had a mistranscribed vessel name.
15. For Elm, all 60 reviewed vessel names matched the originals.
16. For Elm, 8 of the 60 reviewed entries were placed under the wrong year.
17. Rowan passed both the vessel-name and chronological-order checks in all 60 reviewed records.
18. The Rowan review result is not evidence that all 300 Rowan records are correct.
19. Cedar and Elm failed different checks.
20. The committee’s first dashboard marked a collection green whenever either check passed.
21. On 1 July, the dashboard rule was changed to require both checks.
22. The source identifies the dashboard change as a reporting-rule correction, not an improvement to the underlying records.
23. Nine Cedar names and eight Elm dates were corrected on 8 July.
24. Reviewers rechecked those 17 corrected entries.
25. Reviewers did not recheck all 180 reviewed entries or the remaining 720 catalogue records.
26. The limited recheck supports correction of those observed entries, not complete certification.
27. Rights clearance is complete for 760 of the 900 scans.
28. The remaining 140 scans are held internally pending review.
29. Catalogue descriptions for those 140 records are visible, but their page images are not publicly downloadable.
30. The rights team expects to review the remaining scans next quarter; the source describes this as a forecast without a committed release date.
31. The committee approved designing a deeper random audit stratified by handwriting, print quality and collection.
32. The committee has not approved a full-archive rollout.
33. The committee has not approved a claim that assisted cataloguing outperforms manual work.
34. The proposed audit would assess names, chronology, translation roles, publication rights and access separately.
35. The proposed audit design has not been tested and no new pass rate exists.
36. For communication, the committee wants the pilot’s 900 catalogue entries acknowledged.
37. For communication, the committee wants the specific corrections to observed errors acknowledged.
38. For communication, the committee wants distinct quality dimensions and the publication boundary to remain visible.

### Unknowns

- Whether all 900 catalogue records have accurate descriptions or correct chronological placement is not established. The reviewed records were a non-random selection, and the recheck covered only the 17 corrected entries.
- Whether all 900 scans are publicly downloadable is not established as true; the source states that images for 140 records are not publicly downloadable.
- Whether the archive is entirely error-free is not established; the observed corrections and limited recheck do not certify the full catalogue.
- The source does not establish a full-archive rollout decision; it states that the committee has not approved one.
- The effects of the bilingual catalogue on users are unmeasured in the source.
- Whether the distinct checks or corrections amount to complete quality across the full holdings is unknown.
