### Unknowns

- Whether the 560 unsampled strings contain the same or other terminology or semantic issues; the source says the sample results do not certify all 640 strings.
- Whether the form as a whole meets any general accessibility standard; no general accessibility certification was commissioned.
- Whether the upload defect was fixed after the recorded status; no deployed fix is recorded, and the source does not give a later status.
- Whether the approved keyboard-fix proposal was implemented.
- Whether task completion would differ under comparable completion definitions, recruitment, and participant assignment; the reported figures are not a comparable completion-effect estimate.
- Whether the checker would produce stable results on repeated runs; the source says the run counts do not establish a stable checker.
- Whether Polish consent received legal approval.
- The date of the stated Spanish consent approval and its timing relative to the sample review.
- Whether the poll’s 16-of-18 response fraction represents satisfaction in a wider population; the source explicitly rules out treating it as population satisfaction.
- Whether the form increased successful submissions or benefits uptake; the poll did not measure these outcomes.
- Whether the “inclusive launch” ambition was achieved as an inclusion outcome; the source says it is not a measured outcome.
- Reasons for withholding general release beyond the stated conditional authorization of a study and the requirement for a verified upload fix.
- Any cost saving from the study; none is demonstrated.
- The source does not establish a broad finding that the service is accessible or inaccessible. It reports specific test results, including a keyboard activation failure on one tested template.
