### Unknowns

- The source does not establish the sensor upgrade’s causal effect on the alert count; it explicitly says the lower count cannot isolate that effect (Facts 6–20).
- The source does not establish a rate-adjusted treatment effect (Fact 12).
- The source does not establish that the pressure readings received were inaccurate; it says they were not shown to be inaccurate (Fact 24).
- Whether the proposed antenna move would address the packet-delivery fault is not established (Facts 25–26).
- Whether the battery replacement was connected to the delivery check, or repaired the packet fault, is not established (Facts 27–29).
- The separate contributions of the capacitor swap and firmware update to the repeat bench result are not established (Facts 32–36).
- The source does not establish which replay count, if any, is the true count (Facts 37–40).
- Overall field uptime or annual reliability is not established; the bench result does not certify field uptime, and no annual reliability benefit was measured (Facts 36, 47).
- The source does not establish a safety or leak-prevention benefit (Fact 47).
- The source does not establish savings from the budget or study (Facts 44–46).
- Attendance does not establish technician competence (Facts 48–49).
- The source does not state a lesson or establish a general conclusion that calibration alone determines pilot reliability (Facts 21–25, 36, 47).

### Development sequence

- The legacy fortnight recorded 46 alerts in 1,400 logger-hours (Fact 6); the pilot fortnight recorded 29 alerts in 1,120 logger-hours (Fact 7). The source does not establish that the difference was caused by the sensor upgrade (Fact 20).
- The presentation reported 25 pilot alerts (Fact 9); the final ledger restored four suppressed duplicates with distinct device-event timestamps (Fact 10) and recorded 29 alerts (Fact 11).
- All twelve loggers passed the static calibration check (Fact 21). Four loggers failed the overnight packet-delivery check (Facts 22–23); the fault remained open at review (Fact 25). The relationship between the battery replacement and the delivery fault is not established (Facts 27–29).
- A bench check recorded three brownouts (Fact 30). A capacitor swap and firmware update were made before a repeat bench check with no brownouts (Facts 32–34). Their separate contributions are not identified, and the result does not certify field uptime (Facts 35–36).
- Three replays emitted 29, 31, and 28 alerts (Fact 37); the team did not select a true replay count (Fact 39). These replay counts do not replace the final field ledger (Fact 40).
- The committee authorized a six-week paired measurement study with unchanged thresholds (Fact 42) and did not authorize network-wide deployment (Fact 43). The source does not state the committee’s decision criteria or rationale.
