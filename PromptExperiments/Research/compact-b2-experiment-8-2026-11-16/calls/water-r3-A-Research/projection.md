### Facts

1. The dossier is dated 30 October 2026.
2. The dossier describes itself as a newly constructed evaluation source, not a real utility study.
3. The review concerns twelve pressure loggers at two branches.
4. The review is not a drinking-water safety certification.
5. The team called the pilot encouraging.
6. The dossier identifies that assessment as the team’s assessment, not an independently established result.
7. The legacy fortnight recorded 46 low-pressure alerts in 1,400 logger-hours.
8. The pilot fortnight recorded 29 low-pressure alerts in 1,120 logger-hours.
9. The alert counts are not counts of confirmed leaks or affected households.
10. A presentation had reported 25 pilot alerts.
11. The final ledger restored four suppressed duplicates whose timestamps were distinct device events.
12. The legacy alert threshold was 1.8 bar for 30 seconds.
13. The pilot alert threshold was 1.6 bar for 60 seconds.
14. Old traces were not reprocessed.
15. A planned night flushing operation occurred during the legacy fortnight only.
16. Branches were selected for ease of access, not randomly.
17. Rainfall differed between the fortnights.
18. No unaffected comparison branch was instrumented.
19. The dossier states that a lower alert count cannot isolate the sensor upgrade’s effect.
20. The report does not estimate a rate-adjusted treatment effect.
21. All twelve loggers passed the static reference-gauge calibration at 2.0 bar.
22. Four loggers failed the overnight packet-delivery check.
23. Those four loggers lost 17% of scheduled messages in that check.
24. The pressure readings that arrived were not shown to be inaccurate.
25. The packet-delivery fault remained open at review.
26. A proposed antenna move had not been tested.
27. Battery replacement was completed on two different devices.
28. The dossier provides no date linking the battery replacement to the packet-delivery check.
29. The dossier states that the battery status should not be treated as a repair of the packet fault.
30. A bench check recorded three brownouts.
31. The dossier defines the brownouts as transient supply-voltage drops causing device restarts, not a regional power outage.
32. A capacitor swap was made before a repeat bench check.
33. A firmware update was made before a repeat bench check.
34. The repeat bench check recorded no brownouts.
35. The separate contributions of the capacitor swap and firmware update were not identified.
36. The dossier states that the bench result does not certify field uptime.
37. Three replays of the same stored trace emitted 29, 31, and 28 alerts.
38. A nondeterministic deduplication tie-breaker was enabled.
39. The team did not select one replay as the true count.
40. The dossier states that the replay counts do not replace the final field ledger of 29.
41. The dossier states that repeat variability and changed thresholds limit attribution.
42. The committee authorized a six-week paired measurement study with unchanged thresholds.
43. The committee did not authorize network-wide deployment.
44. The budget of EUR 18,000 covers logger rental and field visits.
45. The budget excludes staff overtime.
46. The dossier states that the budget does not establish savings.
47. No safety, leak-prevention, or annual reliability benefit was measured.
48. Training attendance was 10 of 12 technicians.
49. Attendance was not a competence test.

### Unknowns

- The source does not establish the sensor upgrade’s causal effect on the alert count; it explicitly says the lower count cannot isolate that effect.
- The source does not provide a rate-adjusted treatment-effect estimate.
- The source does not establish that the observed alert counts represent confirmed leaks or affected households.
- The source does not establish the overall reliability of the pilot from the calibration result or other checks.
- The source does not establish that the pressure readings received during the packet-delivery check were inaccurate; it says they were not shown to be inaccurate.
- Whether the battery replacement was related to the packet-delivery check, or repaired the packet fault, is not established.
- Whether the proposed antenna move would address the packet-delivery fault is unknown; it had not been tested.
- The separate contributions of the capacitor swap and firmware update to the repeat bench result are not identified.
- Field uptime is not certified by the bench result.
- The team did not identify one replay count as the true count; the source does not establish which replay count is definitive.
- Whether any savings, safety benefit, leak-prevention benefit, or annual reliability benefit occurred is not established; those benefits were not measured.
- Technician competence is not established by attendance.
- The source does not state why the committee authorized the paired study or did not authorize network-wide deployment.
- The source does not establish that the pilot’s alert-count difference reflects a change in leak incidence.
