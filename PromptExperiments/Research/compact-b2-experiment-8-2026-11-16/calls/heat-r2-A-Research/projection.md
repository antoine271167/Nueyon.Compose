### Facts

1. The final metered record reports 410 kWh of useful heat from 500 kWh of electrical input over the trial boundary.
2. A draft listed 450 kWh of useful heat.
3. The source says 40 kWh was duplicated during meter-file concatenation and removed.
4. The 410/500 ratio applies to the trial boundary and period, not seasonal efficiency.
5. The legacy boiler's displayed 76% used fuel energy on a lower-heating-value basis.
6. The electrical trial used meter input and included pump demand.
7. No common-boundary reanalysis exists.
8. Trial shifts processed lighter products than the boiler reference shifts.
9. Ambient temperature was 8 degrees higher during the trial shifts than during the boiler reference shifts.
10. Operators selected the trial shifts to avoid peak production.
11. There was no random assignment or matched control.
12. The record says it cannot isolate a storage-technology effect.
13. The record says it cannot quantify avoided emissions.
14. Grid-carbon intensity was not measured.
15. All four vessels passed the specified pressure-hold test.
16. Two vessels failed the insulation surface-temperature check at the access hatch.
17. The hot surfaces remained unresolved at sign-off.
18. A proposed shield was not installed.
19. The source states that passing the pressure test does not demonstrate acceptable touch temperature.
20. All four vessels passed an interlock test.
21. The interlock test did not test the hot-surface problem.
22. Three thermal trips occurred during initial charging.
23. The source defines a thermal trip as a protective over-temperature shutdown, not a production journey or heat-delivery cycle.
24. A controller setting and sensor mounting were changed together before a repeat charge.
25. The repeat charge had zero trips.
26. The record says it cannot separate the contributions of the controller-setting and sensor-mounting changes.
27. Zero trips in one repeat does not establish long-term reliability.
28. Three identical-input simulation runs estimated usable discharge at 402, 418, and 407 kWh.
29. The solver used stochastic initialisation.
30. The simulations are not three additional metered field trials and do not supersede the measured 410 kWh.
31. The source identifies solver variation as an attribution limit for interpreting simulation improvements.
32. The finance appendix reports a EUR 9,200 rental allowance.
33. The appendix gives no date or sequence for the allowance relative to testing.
34. The allowance excludes installation, downtime, and maintenance.
35. The source says no payback claim is supported.
36. Procurement approved only a longer instrumented trial conditional on a verified hatch remedy.
37. No purchase or factory-wide roll-out was approved.
38. An operator survey received seven responses from twelve operators.
39. Five respondents preferred the quieter charging shift.
40. Noise levels were not instrumented, and trial workloads differed.
41. The supplier's phrase “dependable low-carbon heat” is identified as a marketing interpretation, not a measured emissions conclusion.
42. The source says the supplier's emissions phrase is not validated by the survey preference.

### Unknowns

- The source does not establish a common-boundary comparison between the legacy boiler's displayed 76% and the trial's 410/500 ratio; it states that no common-boundary reanalysis exists.
- The storage-technology effect and avoided emissions are not isolated or quantified in the record; grid-carbon intensity was not measured.
- The source does not establish acceptable touch temperature at the two access hatches at sign-off; the hot surfaces remained unresolved and the proposed shield was not installed.
- The record does not establish the separate contributions of the controller-setting change and sensor-mounting change to the zero-trip repeat charge.
- The source does not establish long-term reliability from the single repeat charge.
- The source does not establish that the simulation estimates are additional metered field results or that solver variation represents a change in field performance.
- The source gives no date or sequence for the rental allowance relative to testing, and states that no payback claim is supported.
- The source does not state why procurement made the longer trial conditional on a verified hatch remedy, beyond reporting that condition.
- Noise levels were not instrumented; the survey preference does not establish an emissions result.
