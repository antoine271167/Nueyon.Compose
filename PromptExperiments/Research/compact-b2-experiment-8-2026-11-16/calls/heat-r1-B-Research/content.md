### Facts
1. The source describes a fictional two-shift factory trial; it is not a safety approval or annual energy model. (“synthetic engineering memorandum”; opening paragraph)
2. The final metered record reports 410 kWh useful heat from 500 kWh electrical input. A draft listed 450 kWh; 40 kWh duplicated during meter-file concatenation was removed. (“final metered record”; “duplicated”)
3. The 410/500 ratio applies only to this trial boundary and period, not seasonal efficiency. The legacy boiler’s displayed 76% used fuel energy on a lower-heating-value basis; the electrical trial used meter input and included pump demand. No common-boundary reanalysis exists. (“Performance and measurement”)
4. Trial shifts processed lighter products, had ambient temperatures 8 degrees higher than the boiler-reference shifts, and were selected to avoid peak production. There was no random assignment or matched control; the record says it cannot isolate a storage-technology effect or quantify avoided emissions. Grid-carbon intensity was not measured. (“Performance and measurement”)
5. All four vessels passed the specified pressure-hold test, but two failed the insulation surface-temperature check at the access hatch. The hot surfaces remained unresolved at sign-off, and a proposed shield was not installed. The source states that passing the pressure test does not demonstrate acceptable touch temperature. (“Successful and failed controls”)
6. All four vessels passed an interlock test; that test did not test the hot-surface problem. (“Successful and failed controls”)
7. Three thermal trips occurred during initial charging. The source defines a thermal trip as a protective over-temperature shutdown, not a production journey or heat-delivery cycle. (“Successful and failed controls”)
8. Before a repeat charge with zero trips, both a controller setting and sensor mounting were changed. The record cannot separate their contributions, and says zero trips in one repeat does not establish long-term reliability. (“Successful and failed controls”)
9. Three identical-input simulation runs estimated usable discharge at 402, 418 and 407 kWh; the solver used stochastic initialisation. The source says these are not additional metered field trials and do not supersede the measured 410 kWh; solver variation limits attribution of simulation improvements. (“Variability, sequencing and decision”)
10. The finance appendix reports a EUR 9,200 rental allowance but gives no date or sequence relative to testing. It excludes installation, downtime and maintenance; the source says no payback claim is supported. (“Variability, sequencing and decision”)
11. Procurement approved only a longer instrumented trial conditional on a verified hatch remedy; no purchase or factory-wide roll-out was approved. (“Variability, sequencing and decision”)
12. The supplier’s phrase “dependable low-carbon heat” is identified as a marketing interpretation, not a measured emissions conclusion. The record also says a sensor serial number was corrected in the asset register, but does not say the physical sensor was replaced. (Opening paragraph; “Variability, sequencing and decision”)

### Unknowns
- Seasonal efficiency and a like-for-like efficiency comparison with the legacy boiler are not established: the trial ratio is boundary- and period-specific, and there is no common-boundary reanalysis (Facts 2–4).
- The storage technology’s independent effect, avoided emissions, and emissions performance are unestablished; the shifts were not matched or randomized, grid-carbon intensity was not measured, and the supplier’s phrase is not a measured conclusion (Facts 4, 12).
- Whether the hot-surface issue was remedied after sign-off, and whether any remedy was verified, is not established; the proposed shield was not installed at sign-off (Facts 5, 11).
- The relative contribution of the controller change and sensor-mounting change, and long-term trip reliability, are unestablished (Fact 8).
- The simulations do not establish additional field performance or explain an improvement independently of solver variation (Fact 9).
- Payback, and the rental allowance’s timing relative to testing, are unsupported by the appendix (Fact 10).
- A physical sensor replacement is not established by the asset-register serial-number correction (Fact 12).
