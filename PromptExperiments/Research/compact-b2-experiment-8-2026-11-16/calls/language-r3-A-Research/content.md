### Unknowns

- The source does not establish why the six Spanish deadline exceptions were reversed.
- The source does not establish the semantic correctness of all 640 strings; the reported linguistic review covers a sample, and the remaining strings did not receive the second semantic review.
- The source does not establish accessibility across all pages, templates, tasks, or users. It reports results for three tested templates and identifies an open upload defect on the second template; it also says no general accessibility certification was commissioned.
- The source does not establish whether a keyboard fix was later deployed or whether the upload defect was resolved.
- The source does not establish a comparable completion-effect estimate between the old service and the multilingual form; it explicitly says the reported figures are not such an estimate.
- The source does not establish that the multilingual form improved successful application submission or benefits uptake. The dashboard used a different completion measure, and the poll did not measure either outcome.
- The source does not establish population satisfaction from the poll.
- The source does not establish a temporal relationship between Spanish consent legal approval and the sample review, or documented legal approval for Polish consent.
- The source does not establish that translation availability means legal approval.
- The source does not establish cost savings from the study budget.
- The source does not establish that the form or service achieved an inclusive outcome; “inclusive launch” is identified as an ambition, not a measured finding.
- The source does not establish an overall finding that the form is accessible or inaccessible as a whole.
- The source does not establish the outcome of the authorised usability study, which was conditional on a verified upload fix.

### Development sequence

- Reviewers sampled 80 of 640 message strings; the source does not establish when this review occurred relative to the other checks.
- The sample included six Spanish deadline reversals; a second reviewer checked those six and agreed. The remaining strings did not receive that second semantic review.
- The source reports access checks: the contrast scan passed on three templates; keyboard-only testing could not activate the upload button on the second template; screen-reader testing announced the label on that template but did not test keyboard activation. The temporal order among these checks is not established.
- The upload defect was still open. Management approved a keyboard-fix proposal; no deployed fix is recorded. The source does not establish whether the proposal was implemented later.
- The old English-only service recorded 33 completions in 50 moderated sessions; the multilingual form recorded 42 in 50 unmoderated sessions. These are different measures and conditions, and the source says they do not provide a comparable completion-effect estimate.
- Three checker runs on the same 80 strings flagged six, five, and six issues; a human reference review identified six deadline reversals. The source says the automated counts do not establish different ground truths or a stable checker.
- The coordinator authorised a corrected-form usability study conditional on a verified upload fix and withheld general release. The study’s outcome is not reported.
