### Facts

1. The final metered record reports 410 kWh useful heat from 500 kWh electrical input over the trial boundary. A draft listed 450 kWh; 40 kWh duplicated during meter-file concatenation was removed. The ratio applies only to this trial period and boundary, not seasonal efficiency. (Performance and measurement)
2. The legacy boiler’s displayed 76% used fuel energy on a lower-heating-value basis; the electrical trial used meter input and included pump demand. No common-boundary reanalysis exists. (Performance and measurement)
3. Trial shifts processed lighter products, had ambient temperature 8 degrees higher than boiler-reference shifts, and were selected to avoid peak production. There was no random assignment or matched control; the record says it cannot isolate a storage-technology effect or quantify avoided emissions, and grid-carbon intensity was not measured. (Performance and measurement)
4. All four vessels passed the specified pressure-hold test. Two failed the insulation surface-temperature check at the access hatch; hot surfaces remained unresolved at sign-off, and a proposed shield was not installed. The source states that passing the pressure test does not demonstrate acceptable touch temperature. (Successful and failed controls)
5. An interlock test passed for all four vessels, but did not test the hot-surface problem. (Successful and failed controls)
6. Three thermal trips occurred during initial charging. The source defines a thermal trip as a protective over-temperature shutdown, not a production journey or heat-delivery cycle. (Successful and failed controls)
7. A controller setting and sensor mounting were changed together before a repeat charge, which had zero trips. The record cannot separate the two actions’ contributions; one trip-free repeat does not establish long-term reliability. (Successful and failed controls)
8. Three identical-input simulation runs estimated usable discharge at 402, 418 and 407 kWh. The solver used stochastic initialisation; these were simulations, not additional metered field trials, and do not supersede the measured 410 kWh. The source identifies solver variation as an attribution limit for interpreting simulation improvements. (Variability, sequencing and decision)
9. The finance appendix reports a EUR 9,200 rental allowance but gives no date or sequence relative to testing. It excludes installation, downtime and maintenance, so the source supports no payback claim. (Variability, sequencing and decision)
10. Procurement approved only a longer instrumented trial conditional on a verified hatch remedy; no purchase or factory-wide roll-out was approved. (Variability, sequencing and decision)
11. Seven of twelve operators responded to a survey; five preferred the quieter charging shift. Noise was not instrumented and trial workloads differed. The source says this preference does not validate the supplier’s phrase “dependable low-carbon heat”; that phrase is a marketing interpretation, not a measured emissions conclusion. (Introduction; Variability, sequencing and decision)
12. The source reports that a sensor serial number was corrected in the asset register; it does not say the physical sensor was replaced. (Variability, sequencing and decision)

### Unknowns

- Seasonal efficiency and a like-for-like efficiency comparison with the legacy boiler are unsupported: the trial boundary differs from the boiler’s stated basis, and no common-boundary reanalysis is provided. (Facts 1–3)
- The storage technology’s causal contribution to performance, avoided emissions, and any emissions benefit remain unestablished; the record lacks a matched/randomized comparison and grid-carbon measurement. (Fact 3)
- Whether the failed hatch surface-temperature checks were later remedied is not established; at sign-off the hot surfaces were unresolved and the proposed shield was uninstalled. (Fact 4)
- The separate contributions of the controller-setting change and sensor-mounting change, and long-term trip reliability, are not established. (Fact 7)
- The rental allowance’s timing relative to testing is unspecified, and the incomplete cost allowance cannot establish payback. (Fact 9)
- Instrumented noise levels and whether survey preferences generalize beyond the seven respondents or differing trial workloads are not established. (Fact 11)
- A corrected asset-register serial number does not establish that the physical sensor was replaced. (Fact 12)
