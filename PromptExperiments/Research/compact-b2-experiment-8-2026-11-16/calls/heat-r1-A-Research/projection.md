### Facts

1. The memorandum records a two-shift factory trial of a phase-change thermal store.
2. The memorandum states that it is not a safety approval or an annual energy model.
3. The final metered record shows 410 kWh of useful heat from 500 kWh of electrical input over the trial boundary.
4. A draft listed 450 kWh of useful heat.
5. The draft figure included 40 kWh duplicated during meter-file concatenation; that duplicate was removed.
6. The 410/500 ratio concerns the trial boundary and period, not seasonal efficiency.
7. The legacy boiler's displayed 76% used fuel energy on a lower-heating-value basis.
8. The electrical trial used meter input and included pump demand.
9. No common-boundary reanalysis exists.
10. The trial shifts processed lighter products than the boiler reference shifts.
11. Ambient temperature was 8 degrees higher during the trial shifts than during the boiler reference shifts.
12. Operators selected the trial shifts to avoid peak production.
13. There was no random assignment or matched control.
14. The record states that it cannot isolate a storage-technology effect.
15. The record states that it cannot quantify avoided emissions.
16. Grid-carbon intensity was not measured.
17. All four vessels passed the specified pressure-hold test.
18. Two vessels failed the insulation surface-temperature check at the access hatch.
19. The hot surfaces remained unresolved at sign-off.
20. A proposed shield was not installed.
21. The memorandum states that passing the pressure test does not demonstrate acceptable touch temperature.
22. The interlock test passed for all four vessels.
23. The interlock test did not test the hot-surface problem.
24. Three thermal trips occurred during initial charging.
25. The memorandum defines a thermal trip as a protective over-temperature shutdown.
26. A controller setting and sensor mounting were changed together before a repeat charge.
27. The repeat charge had zero trips.
28. The record states that it cannot separate the contributions of the controller-setting and sensor-mounting changes.
29. Zero trips in one repeat does not establish long-term reliability.
30. Three identical-input simulation runs estimated usable discharge at 402, 418, and 407 kWh.
31. The solver used stochastic initialisation.
32. The simulations are not three additional metered field trials.
33. The simulations do not supersede the measured 410 kWh.
34. Solver variation is an attribution limit for interpreting simulation improvements.
35. The finance appendix reports a EUR 9,200 rental allowance.
36. The finance appendix gives no date or sequence for the rental allowance relative to testing.
37. The rental allowance excludes installation, downtime, and maintenance.
38. The memorandum states that no payback claim is supported.
39. Procurement approved only a longer instrumented trial conditional on a verified hatch remedy.
40. No purchase or factory-wide roll-out was approved.
41. The supplier's phrase “dependable low-carbon heat” is described as a marketing interpretation, not a measured emissions conclusion.

### Unknowns

- The source does not establish a seasonal-efficiency result or a comparison based on a common energy boundary; it states that the ratio is limited to the trial boundary and period and that no common-boundary reanalysis exists (Facts 6–9).
- The source does not establish the isolated effect of the storage technology or the quantity of avoided emissions; grid-carbon intensity was not measured (Facts 14–16).
- The source does not establish acceptable touch temperature for the two vessels that failed the insulation surface-temperature check; the hot surfaces remained unresolved at sign-off and the proposed shield was not installed (Facts 18–21).
- The source does not establish the separate contributions of the controller-setting and sensor-mounting changes to the zero-trip repeat charge (Facts 26–29).
- The source does not establish long-term reliability from the one repeat charge with zero trips (Facts 27–29).
- The source does not establish that the simulations are additional metered field results or that they replace the measured result (Facts 30–34).
- The source does not establish a payback claim from the rental allowance (Facts 35–38).
- The source does not establish why procurement approved the longer instrumented trial beyond the stated condition of a verified hatch remedy, or that a purchase or factory-wide roll-out was approved (Facts 39–40).
- The source does not establish a measured emissions conclusion supporting the supplier's phrase (Fact 41).
- The source does not establish a broad quality conclusion from the trial as a whole.
