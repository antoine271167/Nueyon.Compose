### Facts

1. The source describes a fictional benefits form and says it contains no real resident data; the “inclusive launch” is an ambition, not a measured inclusion outcome, and no general accessibility certification was commissioned. (Opening note)
2. Reviewers sampled 80 of 640 message strings: 40 Spanish and 40 Polish. All 80 retained approved glossary entitlement terms, but six Spanish strings reversed the deadline-exception meaning; no such reversal was found in the Polish sample. (Sample and linguistic checks)
3. A second reviewer agreed with the six identified reversals; the remaining strings did not receive that second semantic review. Terminology conformity and semantic correctness were separate checks, and the sample results do not certify all 640 strings. (Sample and linguistic checks)
4. An automated colour-contrast scan passed the three tested page templates. Keyboard-only testing could not activate the document-upload button on the second template, and the defect was still open. (Access and task results)
5. On that template, screen-reader testing announced the upload label correctly but did not test keyboard activation. Management approved a keyboard-fix proposal; no deployed fix is recorded. (Access and task results)
6. The old English-only service recorded completion in 33 of 50 moderated sessions, where completion required a final submitted application. The multilingual form recorded 42 of 50 unmoderated sessions, with the dashboard counting reaching the review screen. (Access and task results)
7. Participants came through different recruitment channels and were neither randomly assigned nor matched for prior experience. The source says the dashboard’s apparent improvement is not a comparable completion-effect estimate. (Access and task results)
8. “Case folding” meant normalising letter case; it did not merge benefit cases or applications. (Processing and reproducibility)
9. On the same 80 strings, three runs of the sampling-based checker flagged six, five and six semantic issues. The checker version was fixed, but sample order varied; a human reference review identified the six deadline reversals. The source says run counts establish neither three different ground truths nor a stable checker. (Processing and reproducibility)
10. Legal approval covers only the Spanish consent paragraph; no approval date or temporal relationship to the sample review is given. Polish consent approval is not documented, and translation availability alone does not establish legal approval. (Status and decision boundary)
11. The coordinator authorised a corrected-form usability study conditional on a verified upload fix and withheld general release. The budget includes translation fees but excludes staff time; no cost saving is demonstrated. (Status and decision boundary)
12. A satisfaction poll received 18 responses from 90 invitations; 16 respondents said wording was clearer. The source cites nonresponse and self-selection as limits on treating that fraction as population satisfaction, and says the poll did not measure successful submission or benefits uptake. (Status and decision boundary)

### Unknowns

- Whether the unreviewed strings, or the full set of 640, are semantically correct; whether the sampled checks generalise to all strings. (Facts 2–3)
- Whether the upload defect has since been fixed or keyboard users can complete upload; the source records no deployed fix. Broader accessibility is also not established by the limited checks, and no general certification was commissioned. (Facts 1, 4–5)
- Whether the reported task rates reflect a real change in completion: the endpoints, moderation, recruitment and participant comparability differ. (Facts 6–7)
- Whether Polish consent has legal approval, and when the documented Spanish approval occurred relative to sampling. (Fact 10)
- Whether the poll reflects population satisfaction, or whether the form increases successful submissions or benefits uptake. (Fact 12)
- Whether the project saves costs, including staff time. (Fact 11)
- Whether the ambition of an inclusive launch resulted in an inclusion outcome. (Fact 1)
