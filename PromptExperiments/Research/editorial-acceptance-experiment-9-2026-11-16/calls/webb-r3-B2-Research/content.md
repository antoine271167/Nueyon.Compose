### Facts
1. Webb’s deployed operational configuration was too large for an available rocket, so it was designed to fold to fit inside the Ariane 5 fairing. The source gives Webb’s dimensions as approximately 70 × 47 × 28 ft and the fairing as approximately 18 ft in diameter × 43 ft tall. (“Quick Facts”)
2. Deployment began minutes after separation from the Ariane 5 upper stage. The solar array and high-gain antenna deployed automatically on the first day; the remaining deployments were choreographed by engineers on the ground and monitored via telemetry. (“Quick Facts”; “Unfolding Webb”)
3. The source describes more than 50 major deployment steps and 178 release mechanisms that had to work properly; it identifies more than 300 possible single points of failure that could have diminished or crippled the mission. At approximately one million miles from Earth, a repair mission was not possible. (“Quick Facts”; “In Depth”; “29 Days on the Edge”)
4. The sunshield deployment included releasing the fore and aft pallets, extending the tower between spacecraft bus and telescope by about 2 m, unpinning the membranes, extending the port and then starboard midbooms, and tensioning the membranes. The sunshield had five folding layers, each less than the thickness of a human hair. (“Unfolding Webb”; “Folding For Launch”)
5. In the second week, the secondary-mirror tripod was unfolded and latched, and the two primary-mirror wings were rotated and latched, bringing Webb into its fully deployed operational configuration. (“Unfolding Webb”)
6. Deployment completion did not mean commissioning was complete: the telescope and instruments took several weeks to cool to stable temperatures. Strategically placed heaters controlled cooling so components would shrink carefully and trapped water could escape as gas rather than freeze on mirrors or detectors. (“Unfolding Webb,” “1st Month”)
7. During the first month, the primary and secondary mirrors were unlocked and their movement verified; the final mid-course maneuver inserted Webb into its optimum orbit around L2, and scientific instrument systems were powered up. (“Unfolding Webb,” “1st Month”)
8. In the second through fourth months, the team checked out and aligned the optics. Initial images showed distorted images of the same target star because the primary segments were not yet aligned as one mirror; alignment involved identifying the segment associated with each image and aligning the primary segments and secondary mirror. (“Unfolding Webb,” “In the second, third and fourth months”)
9. In months five and six, the instruments’ modes were calibrated while observing representative targets; the team also demonstrated tracking of moving targets and made Early Release Observations for release after commissioning. The source says that after six months Webb began routine science operations. (“Unfolding Webb,” “In the fifth and sixth months”; “After six months”)
10. The source reports that deployment was completed “with remarkable smoothness,” and that final results exceeded requirements and expectations. It does not provide detailed measurements or independent verification alongside those evaluations. (“Quick Facts”; “In Depth”)

### Interpretations
1. (Facts 1, 5–9) “Uitgevouwen” beschrijft een bereikte configuratie, niet het einde van de ingebruikname: afkoeling, uitlijning en kalibratie gingen vooraf aan routinematige wetenschap.

### Unknowns
- De bron vermeldt niet de precieze datum of het specifieke moment van Webbs eerste wetenschappelijke waarneming. Ze onderscheidt wel Early Release Observations tijdens de commissioningfase van het begin van routinematige science operations na zes maanden. (Facts 9)
- De bron geeft geen afzonderlijke meetresultaten voor elke release, controle, uitlijnings- of kalibratiestap. De gemelde soepele voltooiing en het overtreffen van eisen en verwachtingen zijn bronconclusies, geen in deze tekst onafhankelijk aangetoonde resultaten. (Facts 3, 10)
- De bron beschrijft veel potentiële faalpunten en het ontbreken van een reparatiemogelijkheid, maar rapporteert geen mislukte deploymentcheck of concrete defecten. Dat ontbreken van een gerapporteerd defect bewijst niet dat er geen problemen waren. (Facts 3, 10)

### Development sequence
1. Webb werd compact gevouwen voor lancering in de Ariane 5-fairing. (Fact 1)
2. Minuten na afscheiding begon de deployment; op dag één volgden automatische uitzetting van zonnepaneel en antenne, terwijl de overige stappen vanaf de grond werden bestuurd en gemonitord. (Fact 2)
3. In de eerste week werden de zonneschildonderdelen uitgevouwen en gespannen; in de tweede week werden de spiegelstructuren ingezet, waarmee de volledig uitgevouwen configuratie werd bereikt. (Facts 4–5)
4. Daarna volgden afkoeling, controles en L2-insertie in de eerste maand, optische uitlijning in maanden twee tot en met vier, en kalibratie en afronding van commissioning in maanden vijf en zes. (Facts 6–9)
5. Na zes maanden begon Webb routinematige wetenschappelijke operaties. (Fact 9)
