### Selected Story
Lowell’s shift from textile manufacturing toward heritage-led redevelopment took shape against a long industrial decline, with real consequences for workers. Citizens proposed using the city’s architectural and cultural heritage as part of revitalization; heritage parks followed, alongside the reuse of more than 100 old buildings and a period of rising employment and business expansion. The story’s central point is that this renewed activity formed part of a broader revival, not a lasting escape from economic change: a downturn had arrived by the late 1980s.
