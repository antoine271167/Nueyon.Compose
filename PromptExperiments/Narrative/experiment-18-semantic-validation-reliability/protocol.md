# Experiment 18 protocol

Experiment 17 was a purposive screening on archived briefs. On held-out subjects definitive recall was 2/3 in both conditions, while triage recall was 2/3 in S and 3/3 with source. Source removed one false FAIL and two acceptable REVIEWs. Wolf's species substitution passed both and Webb's chronology had REVIEW then PASS in development. One run per condition, few positives, and potentially overlapping evidence prevented reliability inference.

Freeze 48 synthetic Dutch mini-briefs from 12 subjects disjoint from Experiments 13–17, two matched pairs per subject. Ground truth is independently determined from source evidence before validator results, by the same operator who authors fixtures. F0 preserves meaning, F1 tolerates minor approximation, and F2 changes the focal fact materially. S has a curated handoff, S+Source adds the archived original-language excerpts. The insomnia handoff deliberately omits the odds estimate while the source discloses it. No example labels or identifiers enter the prompt. Two repetitions, with alternating condition order over examples and reversed example and condition order in repetition two. Fresh sessions and no tools. Cap 192 evaluation attempts, zero retries of completed calls. Invalid responses remain invalid and are outside binary metric denominators. Stop on widespread transport failure.

Score each condition separately. FAIL is definitive positive, REVIEW or FAIL is a triage flag, and PASS on F2 is a silent escape. Also review every finding against its exact quotes, scope, explanation, type and correction. A confirmed finding addresses a real frozen material issue, even if its supporting excerpts or correction are inadequate. For actionable evidence all five checks must pass. Report unconfirmed findings separately. H1 compares the two sensitivity measures. H2 requires fewer unsupported findings and false flags with no extra escapes, with ties reported. H3 checks F2 categories and independent subjects. H4 examines review and incorrect blocks in light of the artificially balanced case mix. Attempt denominators are descriptive. Repeated runs and within-subject fixtures share evidence.

Per-condition GO requires sensitivity >=95%, acceptable false flags <=15%, definitive precision >=90%, actionable confirmed findings >=90%, 12 or more independent subjects with at least 24 positive and 24 acceptable cases and all 192 valid calls. Stability requires at least 90% exact repeat agreement and no recurring PASS/flag inconsistency across multiple subjects in any category. A repeated escape is systematic if the same case passes twice or multiple subjects in a category escape. Conditional GO requires at least 90% triage sensitivity and at most 15% acceptable false flags with sufficient definitive precision and evidence quality, with remaining shortfall in stability, coverage or confidence. Otherwise NO-GO. A single condition can qualify. This deliberately high-contrast synthetic cohort cannot establish production performance. Capture examples and compare all four attempts per error. If neither condition qualifies for GO, do not recommend integration.
