- Faithful simplification of the source fact.

- Adjudication: PASS is supported by the full handoff, so escalation to REVIEW would also be unnecessary.

## N19 apollo / actor

- Narrative: Armstrong en Aldrin keerden terug naar Collins in de commandomodule.
- Source: On July 20, Armstrong and Aldrin entered the LM again / the orbit of the CSM piloted by Collins. / Armstrong and Aldrin returned to the CSM with Collins.
- S PASS / PASS and S+Source FAIL / PASS.
- Faithful simplification of the source fact.
- N19/S+Source/1/1 contradiction: The narrative identifies the spacecraft as the command module, while the evidence says they returned to the CSM. The synthesis clarifies that Collins piloted the command and service module and that they rejoined him there; these are not interchangeable spacecraft labels in this account. The command module is the occupied component of the joined command and service module. The narrative about rejoining Collins need not name the service module. This editorial shorthand preserves the relevant destination.
