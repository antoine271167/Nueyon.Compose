# Previous experiments

| Experiment | Evidence used in design |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | All 36 production briefs passed editorial review. Only 26 passed material fidelity. Wolf and Webb each showed five errors in six briefs. Reuse the editorial rubric and keep the two dimensions separate. |
| 16 | A supplementary fidelity instruction regressed to five material faults in 12 regression runs compared with four baseline faults. Reuse FAA and EIA anchors without modifying Narrative. |
| 17 | Original sources improved specificity and precision, with 2/3 definitive recall. Source availability alone did not resolve Wolf or Webb. |
| 18 | Short synthetic tests showed high sensitivity using original sources, with 187 valid attempts out of 192. This motivates separate reporting of natural controls and operator mutations. |
| 19 | 36 natural briefs across 12 subjects exposed no F2, while supplementary archives reproduced systematic misses. Preserve separate historical and newly authored challenge denominators. |
| 20 | Two of eight known F2 were detected and corrected. Six remained and no repair introduced a new F2. Reuse the exact Judge and schema in fresh paired sessions. |
