# Frozen protocol

Use two fresh GPT-6 Luna sessions per eligible brief, with extraction seeing only the full Narrative. Comparison returns source-side normalization and exact original-source excerpts. Aggregate locally. A material contradiction yields REVISE; a material insufficiency or coverage question yields UNCERTAIN; invalid contracts or transport failures block progression as technical failures. Source excerpt matching is a lexical check, with independent semantic review. A materially uncertain result counts toward triage only when it identifies the actual reference issue.

Pair against the unchanged Experiment 20 prompt and schema in matched fresh sessions. Compare per-error matches and brief-level decisions. Development uses distinct Wolf and Webb archive briefs; the other Wolf and Webb briefs remain sample holdouts on previously seen sources. FAA, EIA and Digital remain subject holdouts. New source subjects include production controls and separately labelled operator-authored challenge variants. Do not treat paired variants or repeated historical sources as independent observations.

Phase A requires >=90% matched triage, <=10% acceptable REVISE, no repeated entity or chronology escape, grounded blockers, no systematically omitted critical category, and fail-closed technical handling. Breadth minimum for a positive decision is 10 independently sourced positive subjects with a meaningful instance in each required category, and 10 acceptable briefs. This diagnostic study may establish NO-GO despite insufficient breadth to establish GO. Report positive-subject clustering and limitations. Phase B only follows a passed Phase A. Use at most two repairs per brief with full fresh verification. Apply the prescribed 80% factual repair and zero new F2 gates and the Experiment 15 weighted editorial thresholds, including mean loss <=0.15 and >=90% editorial acceptance.

No automatic retry. Record raw output, stderr, timing and parsed output by call. Keep technical failures separate from truth labels. Use prior evidence adjudications only where exact inputs match and recheck against source. Review additional findings and evidence accuracy separately. No Compose invocation.
