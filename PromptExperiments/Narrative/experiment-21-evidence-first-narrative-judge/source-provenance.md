# Source provenance

Historical complete briefs, Selected Idea, Synthesis and verbatim sources resolve to the Experiment 20 archived case folders at the base commit. These reuse the archives listed in its archived-index.csv. New source documents were checked against the earlier source inventory. The following archived excerpts are curated inputs from official documents.

- Bennu and Apophis are covered by NASA's spacecraft trajectory account. [NASA OSIRIS-REx FAQ](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/osiris-rex/osiris-rex-faq/)
- Magnitude, amplitude and released energy are covered by USGS. [USGS Earthquake Hazards Program](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards/earthquake-magnitude-energy-release-and-shaking-intensity)
- Coral recovery is covered in NOAA's explanation of bleaching. [NOAA Ocean Service](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/coral_bleach.html)

New handoffs were composed for this study from the archived excerpts. Production Narrative generated the initial new briefs.
