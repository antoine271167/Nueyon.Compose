# Independence and interpretation

The operator was aware of all five historical failure types and consulted Experiment 20's archived material-review file before designing the prompts. That file exposes example excerpts and diagnoses for FAA, EIA and Digital as well as Wolf and Webb. Only the two designated Wolf and Webb briefs were supplied in experimental development calls. FAA, EIA and Digital were held out from development runs and case-specific prompt examples. This is a model-input holdout and does not constitute blinded operator design. Wolf and Webb evaluation variants share source passages with development.

The three new subjects had one naturally generated production control and one operator-mutated counterpart each. The mutations deliberately leave surrounding context intact, including correct statements elsewhere that can conflict with the mutation. Results on these pairs cannot estimate naturally occurring F2 detection or rates on the population of content. The three official source excerpts are intentionally short. Full historical documents or curated earlier archives are referenced byte-for-byte as supplied to Experiment 20.

The same model family generated the new controls and assessed the new briefs in separate sessions. Ground truth and adjudication were operator-owned. Historical editorial scores refer to the exact originals. New editorial scores use full briefs and the established rubric. Seven acceptable briefs and eight positive source subjects are below the frozen floor for a positive acceptance result. All technical and uncertainty holds remain in denominators.
