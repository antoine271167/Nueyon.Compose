Webb’s deployment was a crucial milestone—but it wasn’t the finish line on the way to routine science.

The telescope was too large for the Ariane 5 fairing in its operational configuration, so it launched folded. By its second week in space, the secondary mirror and both primary-mirror wings had unfolded and latched, completing that configuration.

Then came months of commissioning. Webb cooled over several weeks. Mirror alignment followed in months two through four, with instrument-mode calibration in months five and six.

After six months, Webb began routine science operations. Unfolding got the telescope into position; cooling, alignment, and calibration were still ahead.
