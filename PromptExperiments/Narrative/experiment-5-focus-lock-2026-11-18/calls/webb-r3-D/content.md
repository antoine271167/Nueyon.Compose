Central Message: Webb’s deployment was only an early milestone: reaching its fully deployed configuration in the second week was followed by months of cooling, mirror alignment and instrument calibration before routine science began.

Story Progression:
- **Context:** Webb had to fold to fit inside the Ariane 5 fairing. Its deployment required more than 50 major steps, with over 300 possible single points of failure; repair at its distance from Earth was not possible.
- **Development:** By the second week, the secondary mirror and both primary-mirror wings had unfolded and latched, completing the telescope’s fully deployed configuration.
- **Endpoint:** Deployment was followed by several weeks of cooling in the first month, mirror alignment in months two through four, and instrument-mode calibration in months five and six. Routine science operations began after six months—showing that deployment and readiness for routine science were separate milestones.

Critical Constraints: Do not imply deployment problems or present commissioning as recovery from a failure; deployment proceeded smoothly. Do not claim specific calibration results beyond the transition to routine science operations.
