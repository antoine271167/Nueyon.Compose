# Narrative Experiment 5 — Focus Lock

Frozen before live calls. B is Experiment 4 B byte for byte. D changes only the system prompt. Central Message is a substantive intermediate result within the existing Content string. Selected Idea sets focus, Synthesis alone licenses claims. No additional model stage. Production Compose consumes the full Content via NarrativeForCompose.

Four unchanged Experiment 4 fixtures, three fresh calls per arm and fixture. Fixed alternating order, six pairs B first and six D first, with both orders represented per fixture. Identical Narrative settings and rendered user input. One real LinkedIn pair per fixture, selected before results (Webb r1, Wolf r2, Digital r3, Lowell r1), balanced order. Use embedded production Compose with identical settings and LinkedIn specification. Posts are experimental outputs.

## Rubric

Manual review of all artifacts. Score each axis 2 if satisfied, 1 for partial fulfillment or a minor lapse, 0 for failure or material error. Record reasons and material paired changes. An entirely sufficient brief scores 2 on all five axes.

- Focus: the chosen Idea governs all beats, especially the ending. D must express one explicit, supported Central Message. Digital must land on the chosen working method in all three D runs. Usage counts may supply context but cannot carry the ending.
- Progression: Context orients, Development adds evidence, Endpoint adds a distinct supported insight. A repeated takeaway or a misplaced pivot fails full credit.
- Details: specific, relevant details give Compose enough material, with necessary qualifications.
- Fidelity: score claims, timing, species, actors, attribution, causal strength and scope against Synthesis. Use archived sources to identify inherited ambiguity or mistakes. Material false, invented or strengthened claims score 0, including elk rendered as eland, a false strict timeline, or unsupported causal credit. Minor ambiguity scores 1.
- Handoff: usable progression, evidence and constraints without format-specific instructions, contradictions or editorial boilerplate.

Check Webb folding rationale and commissioning overlap, Wolf wapiti and limits on ecosystem claims, Digital concurrent work and uncertain 12-week anchor, and Lowell business departures, historical scope and limits on economic causality.

LinkedIn axes use the same 0–2 scale. Attraction requires a specific, engaging, proportionate opening. Readability requires natural, scannable and coherent prose. Message requires the selected focus through the ending without exposing the brief label. Factual accuracy requires support from the brief and frozen evidence. Attribute errors to Narrative or Compose when the artifacts permit it. Check Digital's final takeaway explicitly.

## GO criteria

All calls and byte-exact offline replay complete. D focus at least 11/12 and above B if B scores below 11/12, otherwise at least equal to B. Digital D passes focus on all 3 briefs. D progression, details and handoff each reach 12/12. D has at least 10/12 fully sufficient and at least 2/3 per case. Fidelity reaches at least B's count of 2s and total score. Any new paired downgrade to 0 on any axis, or any novel material fidelity error, blocks GO. Record all paired declines. Narrative total tokens for D at most 110% of B, with stage and arm usage reported separately.

LinkedIn D gets 2 for message in all four posts, including Digital's method ending. D gets 2 for factual accuracy in all four, and at least 3/4 each for attraction and readability with no paired decline to 0. Every condition is required. An incomplete or invalid run provides no GO evidence. No promotion, commit or push.
