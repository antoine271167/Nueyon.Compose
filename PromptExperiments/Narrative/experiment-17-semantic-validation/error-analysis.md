All ten findings reproduced verbatim Narrative and evidence excerpts. Verbatim grounding alone did not prevent erroneous judgments about severity, context or uncertainty. Each of the four confirmed material findings gave a supported correction. Across evaluation findings of all severities, 2/5 with S and 3/3 with S+Source were actionable under the strict audit. The useful FAA review remains a false negative at the FAIL threshold. Full evidence found no additional confirmed material error.

With FAIL or REVIEW treated as a manual queue, evaluation workload would be 5/16 for S (two material cases) and 3/16 for S+Source (three material cases). This is a descriptive sensitivity check on a purposive cohort with three positive subjects. It leaves the frozen recall and decision unchanged.

If the archived Digital F1 label were retained, evaluation recall would be 1/2 in both conditions and precision would be 1/3 with S and 1/2 with S+Source. The timing excerpt supporting the revised label was reviewed before any model call. No positive examples of reversed actors or a firm false causal assertion occurred in the selected cohort, so detection of those error types remains unmeasured.
