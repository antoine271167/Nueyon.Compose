**Selected Story**
Webb’s compact launch design required it to unfold in space, but reaching its deployed configuration was only one step toward routine science. The telescope completed that unfolding in its second week; cooling, mirror alignment and instrument calibration continued across a six-month commissioning period.

**Story Progression**
- **Context:** Webb had to fold to fit inside the Ariane 5 fairing because its operational configuration was too large for the available rocket.
- **Development:** Deployment involved more than 50 major steps and over 300 possible single points of failure. By the second week, the secondary mirror and both primary-mirror wings had unfolded and latched, completing the deployed configuration. The telescope and instruments then cooled for several weeks; mirror alignment followed in months two through four, and instrument-mode calibration in months five and six.
- **Endpoint:** Routine science operations began after six months of commissioning, showing the gap between completing deployment and making Webb ready for routine science.
