# Architecture

A source-only operator annotation records one material relationship per subject and preserves its exact evidence. The experiment tests comparison with that annotation supplied. It leaves automatic invariant creation and full claim discovery as open operational dependencies. The deterministic stage checks a narrow scale fingerprint and discovers snippets by source-derived anchors. The hybrid adds a fresh comparison of the preserved relation and at most three exact brief excerpts. The Experiment 20 baseline receives its original full input. Confirmed contradiction is FAIL, evidence uncertainty is REVIEW, and invalid execution is TECHNICAL_FAILURE. No outcome rewrites the brief.
