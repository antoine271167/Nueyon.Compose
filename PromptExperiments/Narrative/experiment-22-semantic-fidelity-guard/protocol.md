# Frozen protocol

Wolf and Webb are development subjects. Archived EIA, FAA and Digital are historical holdouts with operator familiarity. Ten independent new source subjects get three unmodified production Narrative generations and one deliberate mutation each. Mutations are a separate challenge cohort. No results from the comparison models can inform fixture adjudication. Candidate selection is source-scoped, shared by B and C, and limited to three matching brief lines. A PASS covers evaluated candidates only. B sends candidate assertions without a definitive mechanical result to REVIEW. Missing candidates also produce REVIEW because coverage is unresolved. A and C run independent fresh sessions. The first response is primary and selected repeat responses are never substituted. A uses the Experiment 20 Judge and decoder unchanged.

GO requires at least 90% exact material matches or relevant unresolved flags, at least 90% confirmed finding precision, at most 10% false FAIL on F0/F1, no recurring silent entity or temporal escape, paired improvement, fail-closed execution, editorial freedom, acceptable burden and adequate category and subject coverage. Individual rules need no observed false material contradiction, traceable evidence and more than one source to establish generalization. Report all gates using natural and challenge denominators separately. Limited natural coverage permits conditional conclusions only when the observed checks show value. An observed lack of benefit, recurring misses or disproportionate burden supports NO-GO. Use INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE if evaluation is incomplete enough to preclude credible comparison.

A true or risk match must identify the same relationship and offending assertion, with a verbatim anchor and grounded explanation. Category coincidence does not qualify. Unrelated flags and failures earn zero credit. Editorial rubric weights are direction 25%, development 25%, Compose usability 25%, endpoint 15%, details 10%. No repair calls. Track acceptable withheld briefs and their editorial assessments.

Historical cases were selected with known errors and source facts. New source facts and deliberate mutations share one fixture author. No held-out labels, generated texts or mutation strings enter a guard request. The rule and prompt are fixed before examining evaluation outputs.
