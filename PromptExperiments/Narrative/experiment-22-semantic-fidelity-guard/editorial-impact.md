# Editorial impact

The experiment preserves the original brief bytes and emits correction directions only for confirmed contradictions. It issues no edits or prompt instructions to Narrative. Compose was not executed. A gate can still reduce editorial freedom indirectly through incorrect blocks or a high manual-review burden.

The source-only independent review hid case origin, guard outputs, expected label, and cohort. It used the Experiment 15 weights, 1–5 scale and 3.50 threshold. The first three criteria must each score at least 3. The independent review is model-based using fresh sessions of the same model family. The reference adjudication is an operator assessment made before comparator execution. There was no independent human reviewer of authored mutations. Review records preserve both reference and second-pass labels. The historical archive retains its original adjudications.

Short source excerpts limit the editorial range. Many new briefs repeat a single relation as context, development and endpoint. They can establish that a guard accepts or withholds accurate phrasing on these handoffs, but give limited evidence about rich stories with multiple independent claims. The study did not test rewrites, channel polish, or the quality of eventual articles. The Digital acceptable-control dispute is described in false-positive-analysis.md.
