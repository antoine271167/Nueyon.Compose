# Prior evidence

- Experiment 15 revised the editorial rubric. All 36 production briefs in its sample passed editorial-only acceptance, with ten material fidelity errors concentrated in Wolf and Webb.
- Experiment 16 tried a small prompt augmentation and regressed on the focal subjects. The candidate retained editorial quality.
- Experiment 17 compared validation with and without original evidence. Both configurations found two of three material errors on held-out full briefs.
- Experiment 18 obtained high scores on synthetic pairs. Experiment 19 found zero natural F2 examples in 36 new briefs and observed repeat instability and recurrent archival misses.
- Experiment 20 found and repaired two of eight known F2 cases. Six material defects survived the loop. The archived Judge prompt, schema and decoder are the Experiment 22 comparator.
- Experiment 21 found 3/9 material errors versus 6/9 for the paired Experiment 20 Judge. Correct source selection sometimes preceded loss of referent or event semantics during interpretation.

The individual study reports, metrics and error analyses are adjacent under the Narrative experiment root. Their denominators differ from Experiment 22.
