# Mechanical rules

A numeric block requires an exact two-coefficient fingerprint in the same order, an explicit shared unit, and a strictly recognized English or Dutch ratio phrase with a differing long- or short-scale exponent. Decimal separators must agree with the ratio language. The check applies only to the annotated quantity relation. Unit conversion, independent numbers, group separators and approximate values fall through. Both snippets remain attached.

Entity and temporal markers discover candidates and cannot prove relationship binding. The Wolf mapping supplies disambiguating referents conditional on context. A marker, omission or substituted word alone never produces FAIL. The prototype has no safe general mechanical entity or chronology blocker.
