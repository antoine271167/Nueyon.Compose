GRACE Follow-On launched May 22, 2018. Twin satellites follow each other about 137 miles or 220 kilometers apart. As the pair approaches a larger mass concentration, stronger gravity acts on the leading satellite first, initially pulling it farther from the trailing one. The changing inter-satellite distance is a raw measurement used to infer shifts in mass. The mission follows changes involving groundwater, lakes and rivers, soil moisture, glaciers, ice sheets and changes in sea level attributable to water added to the oceans.
