Lava from the 2018 lower East Rift Zone eruption covered 35.5 square kilometers on Hawaiʻi Island, including 875 acres of newly created land. More than 700 structures and about 48 kilometers of road were covered. Some properties that lava did not cover nonetheless became inaccessible because lava cut their access routes. Frequent earthquakes near Kīlauea's summit damaged facilities and the Hawaiian Volcano Observatory abandoned buildings near the summit.
