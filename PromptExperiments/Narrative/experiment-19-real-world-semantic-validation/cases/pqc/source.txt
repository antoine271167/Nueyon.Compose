The Secretary of Commerce approved three FIPS for post-quantum cryptography in August 2024. FIPS 203 specifies a lattice-based mechanism for establishing a shared secret. FIPS 204 specifies lattice-based digital signatures derived from CRYSTALS-Dilithium. FIPS 205 specifies stateless hash-based signatures derived from SPHINCS+. Signatures can identify unauthorized modification and authenticate the signatory. When the notice was published, NIST was developing a FALCON-derived signature standard as another option.
