# Source provenance

Public primary sources retrieved 2026-11-25. Each source.txt is a faithful paraphrased extract of the linked original, preserving the relationships used in this study. It is the exact source text supplied to the validator. Narrative sees only the manually prepared Selected Idea and Synthesis from cli-input.json. The evidence section carries the selected facts into the handoff. The central insight and emphasis are editorial interpretation. Manual fixtures may be cleaner and more repetitious than naturally generated Synthesis.

- **coral**. NOAA Coral Reef Watch. Exposure area is not the share of reefs that bleached or died. Confirmation date differs from the exposure window. The end date is qualified. [Current Global Bleaching: Status Update & Data Submission](https://www.coralreefwatch.noaa.gov/satellite/research/coral_bleaching_report.php)
- **pqc**. NIST. The approval is historical. FALCON was separate and under development at the time. Avoid claims that adoption was complete or that algorithms guarantee absolute security. [Announcing Approval of Three Federal Information Processing Standards (FIPS) for Post-Quantum Cryptography](https://csrc.nist.gov/news/2024/postquantum-cryptography-fips-approved)
- **parker**. NASA Science. Distance is measured from the surface. Distinguish direct image observations from hypotheses about origins. Keep the encounter separate from publication. [NASA’s Parker Solar Probe Snaps Closest-Ever Images to Sun](https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/heliophysics/nasas-parker-solar-probe-snaps-closest-ever-images-to-sun/)
- **kilauea**. USGS. Structures includes more than a count of homes. New land is included within, rather than added to, the covered area. Do not estimate displaced households from structure counts. [2018 lower East Rift Zone Eruption and Summit Collapse at Kīlauea](https://www.usgs.gov/volcanoes/kilauea/science/2018-lower-east-rift-zone-eruption-and-summit-collapse-kilauea)
- **fusion**. U.S. Department of Energy. The target energy denominator excludes facility-wide electricity. Fusion output is not net electricity delivered to the grid. The experiment and announcement occurred on different dates. [DOE National Laboratory Makes History by Achieving Fusion Ignition](https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-national-laboratory-makes-history-achieving-fusion-ignition)
- **acid**. U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. A cap is a permitted total rather than reported actual emissions. Units are not plants. The sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides timelines and mechanisms differ. [Acid Rain Program](https://www.epa.gov/acidrain/acid-rain-program)
- **airmf**. NIST. Companion status does not establish replacement. Do not infer a compliance mandate from the Executive Order attribution or discuss present legal status from this historical abstract. [Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence)
- **labor**. U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Availability and the reference period matter. The populations outside the labor force and unemployed are separate. The figures are definitions, not claims about a particular month. [Concepts and Definitions (CPS)](https://www.bls.gov/cps/definitions.htm)
- **gps**. GPS.gov. The 2007 procurement decision was not the 2000 operational switch. Do not infer a date when every satellite lacked the capability, perfect accuracy or a universal quantified benefit. [Selective Availability](https://archive.gps.gov/systems/gps/modernization/sa/)
- **highways**. National Archives. Authorization and designation are not completion. The six-route toll network was the subject of the study; do not carry that characterization over to all later interstates. [National Interstate and Defense Highways Act (1956)](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/national-interstate-and-defense-highways-act)
- **python**. Python Software Foundation. Keep the claim pinned to 3.13. Free threading is a configuration, and a support declaration is distinct from the work of making code thread-safe. [C API Extension Support for Free Threading](https://docs.python.org/3.13/howto/free-threading-extensions.html)
- **grace**. NASA Sea Level Change Portal. Separation is not altitude. Gravitational effect precedes the spacing change. Avoid treating the instrument as a direct camera for groundwater or extending the sea level claim to thermal expansion. [Gravity Recovery and Climate Experiment Follow-On (GRACE-FO)](https://sealevel.nasa.gov/missions/grace-fo)
