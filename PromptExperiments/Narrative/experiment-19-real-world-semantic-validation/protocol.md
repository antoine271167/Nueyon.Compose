# Experiment 19 protocol

Primary corpus is 12 independent subjects with three independent production Narrative CLI invocations per subject. Run in three waves, reversing subject order in wave two. A separate preflight on the already frozen Experiment 16 Digital fixture is excluded, as is the Copilot READY probe. Use the default production prompt resolution, current GPT-6 Luna configuration and independent processes. No upstream or Compose calls. Manually prepared handoffs are marked in provenance. The corpus is in English. Archive all attempts. Retry a failed Narrative at most once for malformed output, timeout, connection or transient HTTP failure; terminate on persistent or deterministic failures. No validator retries. Every scheduled validator call has two independent repetitions with byte-identical input; reverse order for the second wave.

Before validation, review all complete JSON against original sources and both handoffs. Grade F0 faithful, F1 minor and F2 material. Record exact citations and correction directions for F1/F2. Hash the reviewed labels and Narrative outputs before validator execution. Keep labels out of model input. A single operator can review if no second reviewer is available. Correct frozen labels only in a documented addendum with source support and both sets of metrics. If fewer than eight F2 or fewer than four positive subjects, consider archived natural F2 regressions from Experiments 13–17 separately; under eight F2 always run this supplementary set.

Count FAIL as definitive positive, FAIL or REVIEW as triage; PASS on F2 is silent escape. Compute per-attempt rates on valid responses and show planned-attempt counts and failures separately. Definitive recall uses all valid F2 attempts. Compute stability over jointly valid pairs and show pair coverage and PASS/flag disagreements on material errors. Give risk of repeated escapes by case and across subjects. Assess all findings for exact Narrative quotation, relevant exact evidence, correct reasoning and severity, and supported correction direction. The evidence quality gate uses all material findings, with no denominator reported as undefined. Track operational latency, status/workload and invalid JSON separately from other schema errors and transport.

GO requires sensitivity ≥95%, acceptable flag rate ≤10%, FAIL precision ≥90%, actionable material evidence ≥90%, no recurring silent escapes, exact repeat agreement ≥90%, no repeated PASS/flag instability on F2, no invalid or missing validation, ≥8 F2 across ≥4 subjects, and ≥12 subjects with ≥36 Narratives. Recurring means the same material issue escapes across independent Narratives or both attempts on an affected brief; repeated instability means PASS/flag disagreements on more than one material brief. CONDITIONAL GO applies to target behavior without serious recurring misses when positive coverage or confidence is limited. NO-GO applies to demonstrated material misses, false-positive, evidence, stability or operational failures. INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE applies when detection cannot be judged and no demonstrated failure settles the decision. Choose one recommendation A, B or C; report uncertainty and do not start another experiment.

Run archive dates, hashes and status derive from the recorded files. Provider defaults mean fresh repetitions can differ.
