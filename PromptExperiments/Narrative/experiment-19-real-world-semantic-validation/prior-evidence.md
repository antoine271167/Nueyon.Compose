# Prior evidence

- Experiment 15 found 36/36 production briefs editorially acceptable, while only 26/36 passed the material-fidelity gate. The entity and chronology regressions each appeared in five of six production briefs for the respective subjects.
- Experiment 16 rejected the additional prompt instructions. The candidate incurred five material errors across 12 regression runs, versus four for production. Four holdouts were clean for the candidate, but did not offset the regression failures.
- Experiment 17 found 66.7% definitive recall on held-out cases and problematic behavior on Wolf and Webb. Original documents improved evidence availability and precision.
- Experiment 18 found 187 valid results from 192 planned calls on synthetic short texts. The original-source condition yielded 100% triage sensitivity, 97.9% definitive precision, 4.2% acceptable flagging and 95.7% repeat agreement. The decision was CONDITIONAL GO and the natural-output question remained open.

The new source pool excludes the archived 13–18 subjects. In particular, OSIRIS-REx, DART, Census apportionment and the 19th Amendment were checked and excluded before freezing.
