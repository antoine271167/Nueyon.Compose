# NIST study of face recognition software

Source: https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2019/12/nist-study-evaluates-effects-race-age-sex-face-recognition-software

Locally retained evidence abstract (paraphrased from the cited official source).

NIST measured the performance of algorithms from different developers. In a false positive the software matches photos of different people; in a false negative it fails to match photos of the same person. For one-to-one matching, false positives often differed by demographic group and by algorithm; across the study performance varied widely. NIST did not test causes. The relation between performance and training data was raised as a possible topic for further study.
