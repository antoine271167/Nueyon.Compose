# National Archives 19th Amendment

Source: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/19th-amendment

Locally retained evidence abstract (paraphrased from the cited official source).

Some states had adopted woman suffrage before the US federal amendment. New York adopted it in 1917. Congress passed the amendment June 4, 1919, and Tennessee supplied the decisive ratification August 18, 1920. Secretary of State Bainbridge Colby certified ratification August 26. The amendment prohibits denial or abridgment of the right to vote on account of sex. The federal process took place while state-level suffrage was already in effect in some states; barriers to voting persisted for many women.
