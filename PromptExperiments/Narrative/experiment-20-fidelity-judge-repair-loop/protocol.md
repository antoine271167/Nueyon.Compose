# Frozen protocol

Pair A and B on the same initial brief. A retains it. B runs Judge, an evidence-led repair only for confirmed blockers, and fresh reassessment. Allow two repairs, no technical retries, four parallel pairs and a 180-second call timeout. Technical failures terminate a pair and preserve the last complete brief. GO terminates provisionally, UNCERTAIN terminates for review, and a third REVISE terminates unresolved. Repeated briefs on one subject are repeated observations. Historical and new results are separate.

Judge JSON has exact keys and types defined in judge-schema.json. Reject duplicate keys, extraneous keys, invalid enums, blank fields, excess blockers, and excerpts absent verbatim from the supplied texts. GO requires zero blockers; REVISE requires a confirmed blocker; UNCERTAIN requires exclusively uncertain blockers. Confirmed blockers require a nonblank correction; uncertain blockers require null. The five category audit fields are required; use a brief relationship trace or state why the category is inapplicable. Only confirmed blockers are passed to the repair stage. Accept a single enclosing JSON code fence as transport framing.

Use the Experiment 15 source-first rubric. F0 is faithful, F1 is a limited imprecision without a material change, F2 changes an important fact or relationship. Rate direction, development, Compose usability, resolution and concrete details as integers 1–5 using the anchors unusable, substantial rework, acceptable, strong, distinctive. Use weights 25%, 25%, 25%, 15%, 10%. Editorial pass requires total at least 3.5 and each of the first three at least 3. Preserve exact evidence and Narrative excerpts. Score without Judge verdict or iteration labels. Obtain a fresh second review for F2, disputed findings, all repairs and all borderline scores (3.35–3.65 or a core score of 2); record disagreements and adjudicate against source. Prior archive grades select cases and do not determine experimental labels.

Detection is brief-level true positive only when a confirmed blocker matches a reviewed material issue. Report triage including matched UNCERTAIN separately. Report false-positive REVISE among initial F0/F1, silent F2 GO, repair conversion from F2 to F0/F1, newly introduced F2 including in intermediate repairs, and any unresolved outcome. Report paired editorial change and subject-level recurrence. Archive usage when available and record wall times independently from review.

GO requires at least 90% known F2 detected or explicitly UNCERTAIN, at least 80% initial F2 corrected, zero new F2, at most 10% F0/F1 false REVISE, at least 90% loop-accepted editorial pass, paired mean editorial drop at most 0.15, no recurring undetected material category or systematic direction/development degradation, full reproducibility, minimum coverage of 12 subjects, 36 briefs, eight F2, 20 F0/F1, eight new subjects and benefit on at least two independent subjects. Define recurring as at least two silent F2 misses of the same category and systematic degradation as a paired mean loss exceeding 0.15 in either core direction/development criterion. NO-GO for repeated misses, introduced material errors, excessive false-positive rewrites, substantial editorial loss, or no meaningful benefit. CONDITIONAL GO only where outputs improve and retain editorial quality with insufficient coverage, stability or reliability for integration.
