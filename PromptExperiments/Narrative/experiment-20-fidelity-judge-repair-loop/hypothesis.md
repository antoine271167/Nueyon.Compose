# Hypothesis

Explicit factual relationship checks plus bounded, evidence-led repairs reduce F2 relative to each corresponding initial brief while retaining Experiment 15 editorial quality.

Experiments 15 and 16 established the editorial baseline and showed that a general fidelity instruction failed the Wolf and Webb regressions. Experiment 17 missed natural errors, Experiment 18 did better on synthetic edits, and Experiment 19 found repeated archived misses despite 35 F0, one F1 and zero F2 fresh briefs. This design records a relationship-by-relationship audit before any verdict and measures repaired text separately. Idea and Research judge patterns inform the capped structured diagnoses and consistency checks. The Idea validation loop informs the fresh context and exhaustion handling.
