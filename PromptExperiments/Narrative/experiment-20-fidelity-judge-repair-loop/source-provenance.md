# Source provenance

The dataset manifest records each input and original. Archive selection uses five known failures carried forward from Experiment 19, three additional F2 cases from Experiment 15 and one editorially accepted Experiment 15 production brief per selected control subject. Original archived evidence is copied byte-for-byte, including curated fixtures where the prior experiment used them.

The eight fresh subjects come from eight NOAA National Ocean Service fact pages. Each snapshot begins with the source URL. The article body was extracted from the heading to the sidebar or end-of-body marker, with HTML markup removed and whitespace normalized. Source snapshots may contain captions or ancillary text. The handoffs were prepared directly from the frozen evidence without calling any upstream agents. All new subjects are within ocean and coastal science, and shared domain and publisher limit breadth.
