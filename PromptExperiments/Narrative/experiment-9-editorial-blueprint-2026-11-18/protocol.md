# Narrative Experiment 9 — Editorial Blueprint

## Design

Experiment 7 F improved focus and separated outcomes from the editorial conclusion, but invented Webb sequence and once Digital causality. Experiment 8 G added a fidelity guard and retained the conclusion requirement. Both arms repeated the Webb failure and neither reached Compose. Experiment 9 compares the exact archived F with one compact H system prompt. The user prompt is byte-identical. Selected Idea supplies focus and audience. Synthesis alone supplies claims. The frozen source and projection are for audit only. H gives Compose supported meaning and direction; production Compose writes the ending without introducing meaning.

Three independent stateless calls per case and arm (24 Narrative) on gpt-6-luna, identical settings within stage and existing NarrativeResult. The schedule counterbalances first arm 6/6. Each successful fidelity-safe brief is eligible for its own production LinkedIn Compose call, even with soft scores below 2 and regardless of its counterpart. No selection by editorial score, no extra judging calls, no retries or substitutions. Log every exclusion and compare downstream scores on complete safe pairs; report all singletons separately. Compose uses the stock agent, prompt resources and LinkedIn format. Stop after the current request with PAUSE or interrupt it with Ctrl+C.

## Rubric

Score each dimension 0 for materially wrong/absent, 1 for partial or weak, 2 for sufficient. Record reasons with textual evidence. Failure to complete has no score and counts against coverage. Score all Narrative before any Compose call and record the gate. Assess the full brief and the implied meaning of transitions, contrasts and summaries.

- Narrative focus: one clear supported message aligned to Selected Idea.
- Narrative progression: useful context and development with distinct editorial functions.
- Narrative evidence: concrete case details support the message. Optional omissions matter only when they weaken the story. Observed outcomes remain evidence.
- Narrative direction: usable, supported tension, contrast or direction gives the ending a distinct function. Score the meaning available to Compose, irrespective of headings or polished closing copy.
- Narrative handoff: coherent, actionable semantic input with essential constraints and no publishing-format lock-in.
- Fidelity for both stages: 2 means materially supported throughout. 0 means material falsehood, unsupported implication or unjustified causal/time/actor relation. 1 means an unresolved possibly material ambiguity. Only 2 may reach Compose. Compose must independently pass against both its brief and Synthesis.
- LinkedIn appeal: engaging opening and enough specificity to sustain interest. Readability: natural language and coherent accessible flow. Message: one clear supported central idea. Ending: grounded tension carried through to a satisfying, distinct close. Handoff: post realizes the direction faithfully and omits planning scaffolding.

## Case checks

Digital: applicant-first then caseworker work and sprint-week distinction. No launch/results causality from sequencing or research time. Metrics optional and observed at five public weeks. Webb: folding to fit Ariane 5, full deployment in week two versus routine science after six months. First-month cooling and week-two deployment are separate windows. Do not imply cooling started after deployment or add a precise onset. Wolf: packs, food for scavengers and/or changing prey, preserve multi-factor impacts and avoid sole wolf causality or a vegetation cascade. Lowell: citizen heritage effort, reuse and later downturn. Preserve the account's historical scope and avoid park-only causation or permanent recovery.
