**Selected Story**
Webb’s path to routine science shows why unfolding was only one milestone: reaching its deployed configuration took two weeks, while cooling, mirror alignment and instrument calibration extended across a six-month commissioning period.

**Story Progression**
- **Context:** Webb had to fold to fit inside the Ariane 5 fairing. Its deployment involved more than 50 major steps and over 300 possible single points of failure, any of which could have diminished or crippled the mission; repair at Webb’s distance from Earth was not possible.
- **Development:** By the second week, the secondary mirror and both primary-mirror wings had unfolded and latched. The telescope and instruments then cooled for several weeks; mirror alignment followed in months two through four, and instrument-mode calibration in months five and six.

**Evidence/Observed Results**
Webb began routine science operations after six months. The deployment proceeded smoothly, according to the source.

**Editorial Conclusion**
For Webb, being physically deployed and being ready for routine science were distinct achievements: the months of commissioning were part of reaching operational use, not evidence that deployment had failed.
