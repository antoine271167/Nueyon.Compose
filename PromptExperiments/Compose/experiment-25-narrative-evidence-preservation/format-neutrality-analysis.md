The LinkedIn check uses one independent generation per condition on Digital, Webb, DART and Git sparse checkout. These are the same A and B Narratives used for Articles. The production LinkedIn prompt and schema are frozen. The LinkedIn rubric examines substance in criterion 3. Its four subject pairs provide a limited transfer check.

The handoff remains structured as plain Narrative content. Neither the added paragraph nor its template imposes a target channel, writing length, finished prose, hook, hashtags or call to action. Source errors can transfer to either format with the same handoff, as the independent factual review documents.
