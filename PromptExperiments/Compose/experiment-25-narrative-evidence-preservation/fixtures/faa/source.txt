# FAA Hot Spot Standardized Symbology

Source: https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/hot-spot-standardized-symbology

Locally retained evidence abstract (paraphrased from the cited official source).

A hot spot is an airport movement-area location with a history or potential risk of collision or runway incursion. The FAA standardized two meanings with three shapes beginning May 19, 2022. A circle or ellipse marks a ground movement hot spot; a cylinder marks a wrong surface hot spot. The stated purpose is to prevent confusion about the meaning or depiction of hot spots.
