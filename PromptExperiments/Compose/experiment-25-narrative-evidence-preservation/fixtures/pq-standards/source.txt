Operator paraphrase of source evidence

The Secretary of Commerce approved FIPS 203, 204 and 205 in August 2024.
FIPS 203 specifies ML-KEM to establish a shared secret between parties communicating over a public channel.
FIPS 204 specifies a module-lattice-based digital signature scheme.
FIPS 205 specifies a stateless hash-based digital signature scheme.
The schemes are designed to resist future quantum attacks.
