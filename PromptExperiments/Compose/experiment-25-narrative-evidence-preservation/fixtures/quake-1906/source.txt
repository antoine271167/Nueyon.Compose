Operator paraphrase of source evidence

One team studied seismic recordings from 96 observatories and digitized and modeled usable data from 12.
That seismic analysis suggested a surface-wave magnitude of 7.7.
A USGS team instead re-evaluated geodetic measurements made before and after the earthquake.
The geodetic approach used ground deformation reflected in angles and distances between benchmarks and gave an estimate of 7.9.
Different methods of estimating earthquake size can disagree.
