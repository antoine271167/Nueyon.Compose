Operator paraphrase of source evidence

OSIRIS-REx returned material from asteroid Bennu to Earth on September 24, 2023.
The mission required at least 60 grams of asteroid material.
The team exceeded that requirement before completely opening the sampling head.
A final pour added 51.2 grams.
The completed inventory amounted to 121.6 grams.
