Operator paraphrase of source evidence

Before impact, Dimorphos took 11 hours and 55 minutes to orbit the larger asteroid Didymos.
Immediately after impact, the period shortened by 32 minutes and 42 seconds, to 11 hours, 22 minutes and 37 seconds.
Over subsequent weeks Dimorphos lost more rocky material to space while its orbital period continued to shorten.
The period settled at 11 hours, 22 minutes and 3 seconds, 33 minutes and 15 seconds shorter than before impact.
The orbit was no longer circular after impact.
