EPA’s Acid Rain Program addresses sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides from the power sector under Title IV. The sulfur dioxide mechanism applies an aggregate cap and allows trading; one allowance covers one ton. Its phase I began in 1995 with 263 initial units at 110 plants; substitution or compensating units brought the phase I total to 445 units. Phase II began in 2000. The final 2010 cap was 8.95 million tons. The nitrogen oxides component was rate-based for certain coal-fired units, began in 1996 and did not introduce a cap or allowance trading.
