# Frozen protocol

Use six development and twelve disjoint holdout subjects. Rerun A through production from the same byte-identical selected Idea and Synthesis as B. Freeze all prompts including Compose, dataset and blinding plan before development. Use zero candidate refinements. Generate two Articles per treatment on development and three on holdout, with rotating treatment order, independent processes, and fixed production Compose. Generate one LinkedIn Post per A and B for each of four holdout subjects. Preserve failed slots and all completed reviews. A transport interruption may resume only unstarted slots. Every independent review uses a fresh process without tools or condition labels.

Before Narrative generation, create a review-only evidence inventory from source and Synthesis. Track distinct relevant source-entailing developments, scope, exact Synthesis provenance, usefulness, and materiality. C presents selected verbatim Synthesis spans from verified useful items, in inventory progression, using the three production headings. Use an independent inventory verification and C factual review. Exclude disputed items from C. C is an operator-designed extractive diagnostic built from model-assisted inventories. Compare selection and structure jointly and interpret C separately.

Narrative editorial weights are 20/25/25/15/10/5 for focus, distinct supported developments, useful evidence preservation, progression, endpoint and concision. Article weights are 20/25/20/15/10/10 for opening, development, readability, focus, ending and voice. Acceptance requires 3.75 and all six ratings at least 3. Score factual integrity separately as F0, F1, F2 or U. Evidence is preserved only when full meaning is present, including material qualifications. Count distinct useful items once. Use subject-mean preservation rates and subject-clustered paired bootstrap intervals (10,000 draws). Compare realized Article evidence against both the inventory and its Narrative. Use the same editorial rubric as Experiment 24.

B gates are at least 20 percentage points preservation and acceptance gains, +0.30 overall, +0.40 development, and paired mean read/focus/ending changes no worse than -0.15 each. Require positive lower confidence bounds for acceptance and development gains, improvement in at least four subjects, corroboration through realized evidence counts and no reliance on length alone. Require more supported developments with available evidence, no material peripheral increase, recurring repetition or loss of central direction. Recurrence means three or more subjects. The maximum additional parse or runtime failures is one per stage and median Narrative process latency may increase by up to 50 percent. Require all decision-relevant factual claims resolved, no confirmed new Narrative F2, no increase in Compose F2 and no amplification or recurring category regression. Require complete independent material claim coverage, working contract, format neutrality including the LinkedIn check, and exact freeze verification. Missing comparisons or review coverage that leave decisions indeterminate produce insufficient evidence. A confirmed mandatory gate failure blocks promotion. Preserve review disagreements and check disputed claims against authoritative source and complete stage outputs. Report findings by origin and separate strata.
