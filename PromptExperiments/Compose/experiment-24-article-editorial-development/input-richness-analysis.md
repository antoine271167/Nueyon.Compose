# Narrative evidence available to Compose

Counts are approximate manual counts of distinct supported assertions available in the frozen Narrative, excluding repeated restatements, instructions to the writer and identifiable inherited errors. A development is a distinct explanatory step beyond orientation. Concrete details are present in every fixture. “Brief” means enough for a short piece with a clear point, with limited room for a fuller article. Richness labels used for metrics were fixed in the dataset manifest independently of these counts.

The design can identify prompt effects on fixed inputs, subject to pairing and sample uncertainty. Thin content across all treatments is consistent with an input ceiling on short briefs. It does not establish that a richer Narrative would improve the output. Where several unused facts or relationships were available, a weak Article may reflect prompt, model or editorial selection.
