https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/golden-record-contents/

Evidence summary (paraphrased):
Both Voyager spacecraft carried a gold-plated record. A committee chaired by Carl Sagan selected its contents for NASA: 115 analog images, natural sounds including surf and whale calls, musical selections spanning cultures and eras, and spoken greetings in 55 languages. Each record was protected by an aluminum jacket and accompanied by a cartridge and needle. Symbolic instructions explain the spacecraft's origin and how to play the record. The audio plays at 16 2/3 revolutions per minute.
