Evidence summary (paraphrased):
Jeweler Harry Winston donated the Hope Diamond to the Smithsonian in 1958. The 45.5-carat gem was sent from New York's General Post Office on November 8 by registered first-class mail in a plain brown package. Postage cost $2.44 and registration cost $142.85. It reached the Smithsonian on November 10 and was presented in a ceremony attended by Postmaster General Arthur Summerfield.
