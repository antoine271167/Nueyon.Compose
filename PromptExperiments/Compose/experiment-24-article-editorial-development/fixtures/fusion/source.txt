On December 5, 2022, a Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory experiment at the National Ignition Facility achieved fusion ignition. The experiment delivered 2.05 megajoules to the target and generated 3.15 megajoules of fusion energy. DOE announced the achievement on December 13. The announcement treats the experiment as progress toward inertial fusion energy and notes the many scientific and technological developments still needed for practical energy production.
