# Supplemental factual evidence

The frozen fixtures contain the original Experiment 23 sources and locally prepared source digests for newly prepared cases. The following checks resolve specific blind-review disagreements by returning to the cited primary sources. These checks were made for factual adjudication and were never given to the Compose runs.

- BLS classifies browsing-only as passive. Its example is “simply looking at job postings without taking further action.” This resolves the missing example in the frozen Labor extract. [BLS definitions](https://www.bls.gov/cps/definitions.htm)
- EPA defines the sulfur dioxide cap by the amount that “may be emitted.” A cap is a permitted total even if measured emissions come out lower. [EPA Acid Rain Program](https://www.epa.gov/acidrain/acid-rain-program)
- DOE records 2.05 MJ delivered to the target and 3.15 MJ fusion output. The boundary does not establish a facility-wide gain or electricity delivery. [DOE budget document](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2023-04/doe-fy-2024-budget-vol-1-nnsa-v4.pdf)
- NOAA identifies its 5 km bleaching alert area product as the basis for the mapped exposure. It calls the fourth event the biggest so far and gives 68.2% during the third event as the previous record. Confirmation and exposure period are distinct. A title explicitly assigning the whole observation period to an event onset carries chronology uncertainty. [NOAA status update](https://www.coralreefwatch.noaa.gov/satellite/research/coral_bleaching_report.php) · [NOAA satellite products](https://coralreefwatch.noaa.gov/satellite/)
- NASA charts cooling during sunshield deployment and describes its effective end during the same months that optics were aligned. The fully deployed configuration in week two was an engineering milestone within commissioning. [NASA deployment and commissioning](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/deployment/)
- The National Park Service identifies the Yellowstone elk as wapiti. The Dutch word eland refers to Alces alces. The original prey data compare elk calves and bull elk. [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/nature/elk.htm) · [Dutch Language Institute](https://anw.ivdnt.org/article/eland)
- The FAA says “three shapes with two distinct meanings.” A circle or ellipse indicates ground movement. A cylinder indicates wrong surface. The two-shape headlines materially change the distinction. [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/hot-spot-standardized-symbology)
- NOAA says, “In some instances, low-frequency sounds can be heard across entire ocean basins.” It separately gives whale calls as a distant sound that can be detected, and describes whale communication over hundreds or sometimes thousands of miles. It does not demonstrate whole-ocean transmission for whale calls specifically. [NOAA SOFAR](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/sofar.html)
- Two calendar dates, November 8 and 10, support the Hope Diamond's two-day postal journey at day-level precision. The object history and museum archive confirm the November 10 presentation. [Smithsonian Natural History](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/explore/collections/hope-diamond-history) · [Smithsonian Archives](https://siarchives.si.edu/collections/siris_arc_402898)
- The National Park Service places the first crowd outside the Inn and demonstrations in the adjoining park and surrounding streets. Centered around the two landmarks preserves the geography. [NPS history](https://home.nps.gov/ston/learn/historyculture.htm)
- CPython documents how the declaration controls import behavior and describes distinct thread-safety requirements elsewhere in the extension guide. Declaring support does not establish thread safety. [Python 3.13 extension guide](https://docs.python.org/3.13/howto/free-threading-extensions.html)
- The 1944 highway designation authorized the mileage. The National Archives details separate later funding and construction, so authorization alone did not complete the network. [National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/national-interstate-and-defense-highways-act)
