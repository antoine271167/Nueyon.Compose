# Frozen protocol

Use the committed Article prompt as A. B adds one paragraph of development guidance. C adds an internal drafting strategy. Reuse the production system, CLI, provider defaults and Article contract. Freeze all fixture bytes and the two review instructions before generation. No refinement cycle is planned.

Development uses six historical subjects with two invocations per condition. Holdout uses the other six, two supplemental historical fidelity cases and six fresh subjects, with three invocations per condition. Rotate A/B/C order by subject and repetition and reverse subject order on repetition two. Score valid outputs independently using one fresh session per review. Editorial reviewers see the final piece, format and audience alone. Factual reviewers see the piece, Narrative, idea, synthesis and archived source evidence. Review each second repetition twice and obtain independent second reviews on F2, U or boundary findings. Resolve material disagreements using cited excerpts. Incomplete or unresolved cases cannot count as clear evidence for promotion. Preserve failed slots without replacement.

Experiment 23 Article weights in score order are 20/25/20/15/10/10. All six criteria are critical. Acceptance requires weighted score >=3.75 and every score >=3. Use valid reviewed scores for quality means and all planned slots for acceptance, with coverage explicit. Fix the development choice before holdout generation using, in order, fewest confirmed new or amplified F2 outputs, fewest unresolved or unreviewed slots, highest editorial acceptance, highest mean weighted score, then B on an exact tie.

Mandatory holdout thresholds against paired A are +20 percentage points acceptance, +0.30 weighted mean, +0.40 development and +0.40 ending. Material readability or focus regression means a drop of 0.20 or more. Check breadth using per-subject results and a leave-one-subject-out comparison. A new defect recurring across at least three distinct subjects and at least 10 percentage points above A fails the editorial gate. Report uncertainty with a subject-cluster bootstrap and treat gains as uncertain if its 95% interval includes zero for weighted score or acceptance.

Require no confirmed Compose-introduced F2 or other material semantic regression, no additional material amplification versus A, and complete factual review coverage with no decision-relevant U. Report known stress cases and fresh topics separately. Contract validity must hold and a loss of more than one valid slot versus A fails the operational gate. Investigate a median latency increase above 25% and a p90 increase above 50%, taking account of absolute seconds and concurrent workload. No cost estimate from length alone. Document token and cost observability. Keep treatment local to Article. An unambiguous pass of every required gate and a preselected winner are required for promotion. No threshold adjustments after evaluation.
