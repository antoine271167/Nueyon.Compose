NOAA and ICRI confirmed the fourth global bleaching event on April 15, 2024. From January 1, 2023 through September 30, 2025, roughly 84.4% of global coral reef area experienced bleaching-level heat stress. Mass bleaching was documented in at least 83 countries and territories. The previous global event ran from 2014 to 2017 and exposed 68.2% of reef area to bleaching-level heat stress. NOAA’s June 2026 update describes the fourth event as likely having ended in 2025.
