Under the Current Population Survey, an unemployed person was not employed during the survey reference week, was available for work except for temporary illness, and made an active job search effort in the four-week period ending with that week or was temporarily laid off and expected recall. Passive-only job search does not count as active search. Someone waiting to start a new job generally needs active search as well. Receipt of unemployment insurance is irrelevant to the classification. The unemployment rate divides unemployed people by the sum of employed and unemployed people.
