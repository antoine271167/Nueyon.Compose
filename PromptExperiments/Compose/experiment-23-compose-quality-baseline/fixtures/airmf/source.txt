NIST released AI RMF 1.0 in January 2023. It was designed for voluntary use and aims to help organizations bring trustworthiness considerations into the design, development, use and evaluation of AI. In July 2024 NIST published AI 600-1, a companion profile for generative AI with cross-sector scope. The publication was prepared pursuant to Executive Order 14110. Its relation to the order concerns development of the publication; the abstract does not describe mandatory private-sector compliance.
