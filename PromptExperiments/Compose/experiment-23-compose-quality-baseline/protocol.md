# Frozen protocol

One unchanged archived Narrative per subject, three independent production CLI calls per format, interleaved with alternating order and reverse subject order in repetition two. All requests start fresh. Twelve purposively selected subjects, with richness annotated before sampling using concrete evidence density and diversity. The archival sample includes familiar fidelity stress cases. A standalone Research result was unavailable for these cases. Use only the frozen production configuration and exact prompts. One attempt per slot, no retries. Stop after any batch of four identical credential, quota, or model availability failures. Preserve all failures. The primary denominator is planned and attempted, alongside valid and reviewed.

Editorial and factual reviewers use separate fresh unattended GPT-6 Luna Copilot sessions. They do not see generation prompts, thresholds, provenance labels, historical conclusions or earlier judgments. Editorial sees format, audience and piece. Factual sees piece, Narrative, archived Selected Idea and Synthesis and source extracts. A second independent factual reviewer checks each rep 2 and every output flagged F2/U. Adjudicate material disagreements from exact evidence. Preserve both reviews and unresolved claims. Same-family machine reviews are independent sessions, with no claim of independent human validation.

Article weights are opening 20, development 25, readability 20, focus 15, ending 10 and voice 10. LinkedIn weights are hook 25, pacing 20, substance 20, progression 15, ending 15 and voice 5. All six are critical. Grade each from 1 absent or harmful, 2 materially weak, 3 functional and ordinary, 4 strong with minor room to improve, to 5 distinctively effective. Opening ranges from confusing or generic to concrete and arresting. Substance ranges from empty or repetitive to rich development for Article and concentrated insight for LinkedIn. Readability ranges from obstructive to effortless and channel-appropriate. Focus ranges from scattered to an economical, clear progression. Ending ranges from abrupt or forced to memorable and earned. Voice ranges from mechanical or promotional to natural and credible. Per-output gate 3.75/5 and every criterion at least 3. Format gate 80% pass, minimum 30 reviewed across all 12 subjects and no single criterion below 3 in 20% or more of reviews. Below coverage is INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE. At adequate coverage, any failed gate is NO-GO. Report observed variation within subject repetitions and input richness.

Audit every substantive claim including relationships in title or ending. Classify F0 preserved, F1 immaterial imprecision, F2 material discrepancy, and U unresolved evidence. Trace propagated errors, new distortions, amplified uncertainty, independently introduced unsupported content, and unknown origins. A source-supported claim outside the Narrative is a boundary deviation even when F0 against source. Preserve exact excerpts, source and Narrative counterpoints, and materiality. Without demonstrated withholding and verification, unattended publication is NO-GO. Any confirmed F2 is a release concern. Report raw counts and denominators. Token usage, actual provider retries, exact model build and cost may be unavailable.
