# Experiment 003 evaluation

**Decision: REGRESSION — not promoted.**

The candidate changed only the Synthesis system prompt. It permitted a company introduction as the central insight, while retaining the rule against a comprehensive product overview.

| Criterion | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Targeted story focus | Improved in Synthesis; incomplete downstream | Synthesis framed Compose as the first expression of Nuëyon’s AI-first vision. Narrative and Compose still devoted most of their content to orchestration, workflow stages, and the product design. |
| Selected Idea alignment | Mixed | The run selected a Compose-first idea. That fits one requested element but does not communicate the broader company ambition, intended services, and longer-term roadmap as fully as the requested introduction requires. |
| Factual integrity | Regressed | Synthesis Evidence says Nuëyon is “building a portfolio” although the source describes an ambition/aim to build one. This strengthens the source’s certainty. |
| Agent responsibility | Mixed | The final Compose text is publishable prose and does not repeat the “This note is for…” meta-text from Experiment 002. It still repeats the central point and gives technical process detail more prominence than the company direction. |
| Completeness | Regressed | The final post does not explain the roadmap beyond Stage 1 or describe the broader types of possible services/products. |
| Relevance | Improved in part | Company identity and Compose appear together as the central relationship. |
| Clarity | Unchanged | The post is understandable, but “strategic intent,” “primary mechanism,” and orchestration wording remain abstract. |
| Focus | Mixed | It has one product-centered thread, but repeats that Compose makes the vision concrete in several paragraphs. |
| Readability | Unchanged | Paragraphs are scan-friendly, but the opening is a conventional company statement rather than a compelling hook. |
| LinkedIn format | Unchanged | It uses short paragraphs; it does not meet the requested strong opening and gives extensive workflow detail. |
| Overall usefulness | Regressed | The target framing improved in Synthesis, but the final post remains too product-technical and the Synthesis claim-strengthening fails the mandatory factual-integrity gate. |
