Compose
-------

Nuëyon is an emerging AI‑first software company positioning AI at the center of its product portfolio. Its first concrete product direction is Nuëyon.Compose — a deliberately narrow, AI‑native orchestration workflow that expresses that strategic intent in a tangible form.

Compose is presented as a staged process for turning ideas plus accumulated knowledge into well‑structured, publishable content. At a high level it follows six phases: Idea → Discover & Select → Research → Synthesize → Narrative → Compose. The emphasis is on building content from researched, organized source material rather than starting from a blank prompt.

The design choice is specific: treat orchestration and a research‑first workflow as the primary mechanism for producing content, and scope the first implementation narrowly so the approach can be explored without broad feature scope. The roadmap lists Compose as Stage 1 of this direction.

That is the core story: Nuëyon.Compose makes an AI‑first ambition concrete by showing one narrow, deliberate way to put AI at the center of content creation—an orchestration that coordinates information discovery and synthesis into a finished narrative.

Compose should be read as an early, directional expression of that intent, not as a launched or commercially validated product. It articulates a clear design direction centered on orchestration and research‑first content creation while remaining an initial, Stage 1 initiative.
