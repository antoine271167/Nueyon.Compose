Selected Idea
-------------

Introduce Nuëyon as an AI‑first company with Nuëyon.Compose as its first concrete expression
Present a clear company introduction that ties Nuëyon’s core identity (AI‑first, product‑oriented) to a single concrete example: Nuëyon.Compose. The piece should explain who Nuëyon intends to serve (other AI‑first companies), why the company exists (to make AI useful by combining intelligence, tools, knowledge and orchestration), and why Compose is the first step rather than a full platform launch.
