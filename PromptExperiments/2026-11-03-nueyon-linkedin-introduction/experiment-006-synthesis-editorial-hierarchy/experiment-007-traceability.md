# Experiment 007 — Traceability diagnosis: editorial drift across Synthesis → Narrative → Compose

**Date:** 2026-11-04  
**Type:** Diagnostic artifact analysis; no generations, prompt edits, code edits, build, or tests.  
**Decision:** The artifacts support a mixed diagnosis: orchestration is already prominent in the baseline Synthesis condition, but the candidate outputs inconsistently elevate it from a supporting/product-design detail into the company introduction’s organizing claim. The earliest candidate shift occurs in Synthesis run 3; in runs 1–2 it first becomes clear in Narrative. Compose preserves and sharpens the Narrative framing. Run 2 also adds unsupported audience groups, validation intent, and performance-like properties.

## Scope and method

Inspected Experiment 006’s archived input, original workflow instruction, baseline and candidate Synthesis/Narrative/Compose extracts for runs 001–003, `metadata.json`, and the execution-ID headers of the six condition console logs. Prompt snapshots and the recorded prompt hashes were used as condition metadata; no prompt files were modified. Baseline runs were the three reused Experiment 005 baseline runs; candidate runs were fresh Experiment 006 full-pipeline executions. The downstream candidate outputs are the three Narratives and three Compose posts; the three upstream Synthesis outputs are traced as their inputs.

Evidence paths below are relative to this directory. Each run extract has its own filename, for example `candidate/outputs/run-002-narrative.txt`; the matching baseline is `baseline/outputs/run-002-narrative.txt`. The source is `baseline/input/Noeyon-Product-Strategy-reconstructed.md`. Experiment 006’s metadata records the common source hash `DB7A9CF4768684C025D02028980E16CCE8805832E1013A5B6098C00D29E8F2D0`, the three candidate execution IDs, and the condition details.

**Classification key:** “Source-supported” means the source brief states the fact or intention. “Inherited” means the wording/concept is present in the immediately preceding agent output. “Model-added/inference” means the phrase or inference is not established by the source or preceding stage. When a phrase is source-adjacent but increases its certainty or prominence, that distinction is called out. This is textual lineage analysis, not proof of the model’s internal causal process.

## Per-run diagnosis

### Candidate 001

- **Synthesis:** Strongest match to requested hierarchy of the three candidates. Primary message and subject retain Nuëyon plus Compose. “Orchestrated workflow” is already in the primary sentence, but explicitly as what Compose is, while orchestration appears as a support detail elsewhere.
- **Narrative:** First clear shift. It recasts the “first concrete direction” as an “orchestration-focused”/“orchestration-oriented” direction. Company identity stays in the opening, but the main point and endpoint are now about the workflow type.
- **Compose:** Faithfully follows the Narrative’s framing; it does not invent new concrete factual claims. “Orchestration-first” is a stronger label than the source’s company-introduction intent warrants. The no-launch/customer caveat is preserved.
- **Baseline delta:** The same pattern exists in baseline 001–003: orchestration-centred language appears early and persists into Compose. Candidate 001 is not a novel failure mode, but retains the hierarchy problem.

### Candidate 002

- **Synthesis:** Primary message is broadly on brief. The “validate a principle” supporting theme begins causal/editorial drift: the source validates the core workflow as a roadmap activity; the synthesis generalizes that into validation of “Nuëyon’s approach.”
- **Narrative:** First unambiguous main-story shift to an orchestration-first design. It also introduces a specific audience list and recasts the product direction as validation of an approach.
- **Compose:** Amplifies Narrative and adds unsupported properties: “testable,” “predictable and repeatable,” and “central design choice.” It does correctly preserve the early-stage/no-customer caveat, but caveats do not cure the invented claims.
- **Baseline delta:** Baseline already contained an orchestration-centred story and a list of audience groups. Candidate strengthens the technical thesis and changes/extends the audience list; it adds validation causality and workflow properties absent from baseline.

### Candidate 003

- **Synthesis:** Earliest and clearest hierarchy break. Primary message and subject make Compose/orchestration the story; company identity and audience are relegated to context. This conflicts directly with the source’s article instruction.
- **Narrative:** Preserves the Synthesis ordering and broadens the opening to a generalized argument about single LLM calls; “testable embodiment” is unsupported as an outcome.
- **Compose:** Continues the general technical argument, keeps orchestration central, and adds `#AI #Orchestration`, reinforcing the frame. It avoids audience and commercial claims.
- **Baseline delta:** Baseline also overweights orchestration. Candidate 003 escalates from “orchestration-focused product direction” to “orchestration-first principle” as the *primary* message, so this is a candidate amplification of a pre-existing defect rather than a cleanly new topic.

## Factual source fidelity vs editorial-intent fidelity

These are separate axes:

- **Factual source fidelity:** Core descriptions of Nuëyon as early-stage and AI-first, Compose as the first concrete direction, the staged workflow, research-grounded content, and orchestration as a design principle are supported by the source. Most candidate outputs preserve caveats about launch, customers, and proven outcomes. Run 002 is the clearest factual-restraint failure because it asserts unfinalized audiences and unsupported testability, predictability, repeatability, and a validation objective beyond the source’s narrower roadmap language. Run 003 contains source-adjacent but strengthened “more than a single LLM call” language and “testable embodiment.”
- **Editorial-intent fidelity:** The source brief explicitly asks the first company-page article to establish company identity before technical details and says not to let architecture overwhelm the vision. On that measure all three candidate chains underperform: candidate 001 shifts in Narrative, 002 shifts in Narrative with a precursor in Synthesis, and 003 shifts already in Synthesis. Candidate 003 is the strongest hierarchy break. Candidate 001 is closest, but the final post still organizes itself around orchestration.

Therefore “factually supported” does not mean “faithful to the requested story.” Conversely, a priority shift alone is an editorial-intent deviation even when the underlying orchestration fact is true.

## Root-cause analysis and confidence

1. **Strongest artifact-supported cause — mixed editorial signals in Synthesis.** The original/source material has both company-first directions and technically salient orchestration statements, including “central to the development of Nuëyon.Compose.” Baseline Synthesis already put orchestration into its central insight in runs 002 and 003, and baseline Narratives/Composes often elevated it. Confidence: **high** that this ambiguity/competition pre-existed candidate 006.
2. **Candidate structural labels did not constrain content priority.** Candidate runs 001 and 002 keep company-level Primary messages, while run 003 labels Compose/orchestration the Primary message. This directly shows that the five headings alone did not enforce stable content hierarchy. Confidence: **high** as a description of these artifacts; not a causal claim about model mechanics.
3. **Narrative transforms adjacent supporting material into story logic.** In runs 001–002, Synthesis has a company/Compose-first primary message, yet Narrative makes orchestration the main point. Run 002’s “validate the approach” supporting theme offers a textual bridge to downstream validation framing. Confidence: **high** for textual lineage; **medium** that it contributed to the shift, because nondeterministic generations do not reveal causal attribution.
4. **Compose tends to preserve and intensify Narrative’s chosen frame.** “Orchestration-focused” becomes “orchestration-first” in 001; validation framing becomes testability/repeatability in 002; the 003 technical thesis is repeated and hashtagged. Confidence: **high** across these three artifact pairs, but sample count is small.
5. **No single-agent-only root cause is supported.** Baseline already shows the same direction; run 003 shifts at Synthesis, while 001–002 shift at Narrative. The evidence supports a cross-stage hierarchy preservation problem with varying first point of visible drift, not a definitive diagnosis of one agent or prompt as the sole cause. Confidence: **high** in this bounded conclusion.

Uncertainty: model calls had no seed or temperature override; the provider alias can change; baseline runs were reused and not interleaved; the archived workflow instruction was not an additional runtime file. These artifacts cannot establish what wording the model internally attended to, whether “AI product builders” came from latent/contextual influence, or which phrase caused an output choice. They establish observed textual precedence, additions, and condition differences only.

## Recommended next controlled step

Before another prompt change, prepare a **small editorial-ordering control** using the same frozen source and unchanged production prompts: define two clearly separated source-grounded input variants that differ only in whether the company-introduction hierarchy is represented as the explicit top-level message versus technical/orchestration material retained as supporting context. Run repeated full pipelines for each condition, archive every intermediate output, and score factual claims and editorial ordering separately. Keep the archived Experiment 006 candidate as a diagnostic reference, not a promoted prompt. Pre-register a gate that fails if the Narrative’s selected story or Compose’s opening makes orchestration the company-level subject, or if audience/causal/outcome claims appear without source support. This would test whether the ambiguity in upstream synthesis/input ordering is sufficient to explain the drift while holding code, schema, other prompts, and runtime constant. Do not infer that it will fix the issue; the control is intended to isolate the observed hierarchy signal.
