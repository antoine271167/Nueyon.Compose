Nuëyon.Compose shows why orchestration — not a single chat — is the company's core product idea
Nuëyon frames useful AI work as a coordinated, multi-stage process and expresses that principle concretely in Nuëyon.Compose's staged content workflow (Idea → Discover & Select → Research → Synthesize → Narrative → Compose). The story argues that the product exists to operationalize orchestration: assigning distinct responsibilities to specialized agents and an orchestration layer that coordinates them.
