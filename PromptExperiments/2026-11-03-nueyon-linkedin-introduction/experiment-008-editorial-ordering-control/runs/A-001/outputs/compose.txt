Nuëyon aims to operate as an AI-first software company building tools and knowledge for other AI-first builders. Nuëyon.Compose is presented as the first concrete product direction intended to make that identity tangible.

Compose is an AI-native, staged content workflow designed around orchestration. Rather than a single monolithic agent, it coordinates specialized stages — for example: Idea → Discover & Select → Research → Synthesize → Narrative → Compose — each stage focused on a distinct piece of the content process. The emphasis is deliberately narrow: establish this core workflow and its orchestration model first, keeping implementation details subordinate to the overall design.

As a design-first expression of that AI-first ambition, Compose surfaces a clear product direction: focus on orchestration and staged workflows to turn ideas and accumulated knowledge into useful, well-structured content. Roadmap items and future capabilities should be read as directional priorities for development rather than finalized commitments or evidence of market launch.

In short: Compose defines an initial, orchestration-centered approach to making ideas and knowledge publishable content — a starting point for how product development will proceed.
