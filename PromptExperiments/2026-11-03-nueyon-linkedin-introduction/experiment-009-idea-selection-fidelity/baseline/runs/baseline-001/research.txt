### Facts
1. Nuëyon is an emerging AI-first software company.
2. Nuëyon has the ambition to build tools, products, and knowledge for other AI-first companies.
3. The company vision goes beyond developing a single application or adding AI features to conventional software.  
4. Nuëyon is being conceived as an AI-first, potentially AI-only company.  
5. Nuëyon.Compose is the first concrete product direction.  
6. Nuëyon.Compose's purpose is to turn ideas and accumulated knowledge into useful, well-structured content through an AI-native orchestration workflow.  
7. The Compose workflow is organized into stages: Idea → Discover and Select → Research → Synthesize → Narrative → Compose.  
8. In the Compose workflow, the "Idea" stage establishes the subject or opportunity worth developing.  
9. In the Compose workflow, the "Discover and Select" stage explores possibilities and selects a focused direction.  
10. In the Compose workflow, the "Research" stage gathers relevant source material and evidence.  
11. In the Compose workflow, the "Synthesize" stage organizes the material into a coherent body of supporting information.  
12. In the Compose workflow, the "Narrative" stage establishes the central story and its progression.  
13. In the Compose workflow, the "Compose" stage transforms the narrative into finished content in the requested format.  
14. The key principle of Compose is that content should be developed from researched and organized source material rather than generated from a blank prompt without a structured process.  
15. The current development focus is on establishing the core Compose workflow with the first implementation deliberately kept narrow.  
16. Nuëyon's intended audience is other AI-first companies.  
17. Nuëyon intends to provide reusable capabilities that can be used across businesses rather than relying exclusively on one-off consulting engagements or custom implementations.  
18. The precise customer segments, commercial model, and distribution strategy have not yet been finalized.  
19. Several product directions and capabilities have been explored beyond content creation, including agent orchestration, knowledge and information processing, social-media automation, and CAD automation.

28. Roadmap Stage 2: Expand the content capabilities (develop additional content formats and continue improving the research, synthesis, narrative, and composition stages).
