### Interpretations
1. [derived from Facts 1, 2, 25, 4, 14] The source represents Nuëyon as an AI-first company that intends to build reusable AI-native products for other AI-first companies, with Nuëyon.Compose presented as the first, intentionally narrow product implementation. 
2. [derived from Facts 5, 6, 13, 18] The source represents Compose as implementing an orchestrated, multi-stage workflow that emphasizes sourcing and organizing evidence before the final composition.
