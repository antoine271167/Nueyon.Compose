Selected Story
Nuëyon is an early-stage, AI-first software company building tools and knowledge for other AI-first companies; Nuëyon.Compose is its first concrete product direction. The story should make clear that Compose is being developed to turn accumulated ideas and research into useful, well-structured content through an AI-native, staged orchestration workflow. The main point for readers to take away is that Nuëyon’s immediate, directional focus is an orchestration-centered content workflow (Compose) aimed at making research-to-content work explicit and repeatable, while the company remains early-stage and its roadmap is directional rather than a set of launched, validated products.

Story Progression
• Context: Nuëyon is an emerging AI-first software company that intends to build tools for other AI-first companies; communications must acknowledge the company’s early-stage status and avoid implying existing customers or commercial traction.  
• Development: Nuëyon.Compose is presented as the first concrete product direction. Describe Compose as an AI-native, staged workflow that develops content from researched source material rather than from a blank prompt—summarize the stages concisely (Idea → Discover & Select → Research → Synthesize → Narrative → Compose) and note that the approach emphasizes orchestration (coordinating specialized capabilities across stages) and a narrow initial focus on the core workflow.  
• Endpoint: The natural conclusion is that Nuëyon’s immediate product direction centers on an orchestration-focused content workflow (Compose) as a tangible expression of an AI-first company ambition; the roadmap and priorities are directional intentions to be developed and validated, not claims of proven outcomes or commercial release.

Critical Constraints
• Do not claim or imply existing customers, commercial traction, launched products, or validated performance gains.  
• Do not attribute decisions, actions, or strategy to an unspecified team, collaborators, or organization unless the source explicitly establishes those actors.  
• Do not present design intentions or a roadmap as implemented, validated, or committed release plans; treat them as directional priorities.  
• Do not claim that Compose’s approach has demonstrated benefits (e.g., productivity, cost, or quality improvements) unless supported by evidence.  
• Do not invent a definition or operational meaning for “AI-only” beyond what the source documents.
