The Eastmere repair collective will open a ceramics table at the community hall on 21 November. Its notice is for householders wondering whether a broken object is worth bringing. People may book a fifteen-minute assessment between 10:00 and 13:00; walk-ins will be seen only if a booking slot is free. The team will look at decorative ceramics such as vases and figurines. It will not accept objects used for eating or drinking, because its adhesives are not certified for food contact.

The assessment is free. If the volunteers think a repair is feasible, they will discuss the likely appearance and the material contribution with the owner before doing any work. Some objects will leave with visible seams; some will leave unrepaired. A cracked vase on the notice is an illustration, not an example from the event. The collective has held bicycle sessions before, but this is its first ceramics table. It has no figures yet for ceramics repaired, objects diverted from disposal, or the life of a repair.
