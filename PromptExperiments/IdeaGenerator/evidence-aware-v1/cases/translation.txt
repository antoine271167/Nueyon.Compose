The North Quay oral-history project has opened a small access pilot for readers who use English or Polish alongside Dutch. Its landing page and six short summaries are now available in both languages. The longer interview chapters remain in Dutch. Each translated summary links to the original chapter and identifies which text is available in which language. A reader looking for the full interview will therefore still meet Dutch text.

The project worked with two paid translators; the published account does not describe an automated translation system. Thirty invitation links were sent to local reading groups. So far nine people have opened the feedback form, and four have submitted comments about finding their way between summaries and interviews. No reading-comprehension task or language comparison has been carried out. The current announcement explains the limited access now available and asks readers for feedback on navigation. Further languages and full translated chapters remain possibilities for a later funding application, rather than promised additions.
