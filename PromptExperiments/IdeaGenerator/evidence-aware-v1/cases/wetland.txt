A seasonal note for walkers at Alder Mere describes this autumn's observations along the east boardwalk and the west bank. Volunteers logged 41 reed-warbler sightings from the east route and 18 from the west. These are sightings, not identified individual birds. The east route was visited on six mornings, the west on three afternoons. Observers followed the same written recording sheet but worked at different hours and did not cover the same distance.

Reeds were cut near part of the west bank in February for access to a drainage gate. No pre-cutting survey is available. The note includes a map of where observations were made and invites walkers to distinguish a place with more recorded sightings from a place known to have more birds. The reserve team has not selected a habitat intervention for next season. It will compare route coverage before deciding whether the counts can inform one. The intended piece is a readable account of what visitors can notice and what the records can tell them, rather than a recommendation to cut or preserve one bank first.
