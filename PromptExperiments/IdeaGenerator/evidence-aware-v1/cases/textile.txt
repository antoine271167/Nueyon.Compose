Common Thread is a new cooperative set up by four housing associations to keep repairable curtains, covers and shared-room textiles in use across their buildings. Its introductory note is addressed to other member housing associations considering participation. The cooperative's ambition is a shared repair service, with the associations setting priorities together. A curtain repair pilot is its first planned service, not a separate technology business.

Two caretakers have entered sample items in a shared inventory. That inventory is intended to help a repairer locate an item and see its fabric and dimensions. It is a supporting tool, rather than the cooperative's main offer. The pilot is planned for January and has not yet accepted repairs from tenants. Members will review the initial work before considering upholstery or other textile services. No price list, savings estimate or turnaround promise has been agreed. The introduction should help a potential member understand the collective purpose and the concrete first step, with the inventory serving as an example of how the service might be organised.
