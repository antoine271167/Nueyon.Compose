Hilltop Observatory has updated the information sent to holders of tickets for its December open evenings. Each evening includes an indoor session about how the telescope works. When the sky is clear enough, staff will also guide small groups through the observing dome. Clouds can mean there is no sky viewing at all. The indoor session still takes place, and a visitor may request one transfer to another available December evening before 15:00 on the booked date. Transfers depend on spaces remaining.

The observatory replaced one camera in September. The trustees approved the purchase in July, and the published minutes give the amount and supplier but no reason for choosing that model. Staff have displayed two images from the new camera alongside older pictures, without a controlled comparison. This ticket-holder update is meant to explain what an evening offers and how weather affects it. It is not the observatory's annual technical review. No claim about improved discovery rates or guaranteed views has been made by the staff.
