The Wren Orchard volunteer circular explains a change to recording this year's community harvest. Pickers will bring each full crate to the shed, where it will be weighed before storage. Fruit still on the trees will remain unweighed; blank entries for an area mean that no weight was recorded there, not that it produced no fruit. Windfalls intended for pressing will be recorded separately from the eating apples. The main job of the circular is to help volunteers use the same record, so the end-of-season account is understandable.

On the first Saturday, the team weighed 86 kilograms of eating apples and 24 kilograms of windfalls. Last year's newsletter gave an estimate of 140 kilograms for the entire harvest, using a different method. Saturday's figures are not a complete harvest total and are not comparable with that estimate. The committee moved the next picking morning from Sunday to Saturday. The meeting note records the change without a reason. No assessment of volunteer productivity or fruit waste has been completed.
