Moorfield community choir is inviting adults who are unsure about singing in a group to a Wednesday taster on 25 November. There are no auditions and no requirement to read music. The first twenty minutes are a shared warm-up; newcomers may listen before joining in. The session begins at 19:00, but someone arriving from work may enter quietly until 19:20. A first taster is free; the ordinary weekly contribution applies from the second visit. The notice is intended to help a hesitant newcomer picture the evening and decide whether to come.

After the October rehearsal, twelve of the nineteen people present answered a short question about returning. Eight mentioned the welcome, three mentioned the songs, and one mentioned both. Two people who had missed the previous rehearsal said late trains had played a part. The choir agreed at its monthly meeting to keep the later entry window through December. The minutes record the decision without an account of why it was chosen. There has been no survey of confidence before and after singing and no comparison with a fixed-start rehearsal.
