An update for regular customers of Vale Street Bakery introduces a four-week evening collection trial starting 24 November. On Tuesdays and Thursdays, customers can reserve a loaf by telephone before noon and collect it from 17:30 to 18:30 at the side door. Reserved loaves come from that day's normal bake. The bakery will not make an extra evening batch. The usual counter and opening hours continue on Saturdays; the side-door window is not available then.

During two informal evenings in October, staff handed over 14 and 11 reserved loaves. Three loaves were left uncollected on the second evening and were donated with the ordinary surplus the next morning. These counts do not include shoppers who used the counter and were not compared with earlier weeks. The baker has not calculated waste or revenue changes. The update's useful job is to make the new collection choice easy to understand for people who already buy there. A review of the trial is planned after four weeks, with no commitment yet to extend it.
