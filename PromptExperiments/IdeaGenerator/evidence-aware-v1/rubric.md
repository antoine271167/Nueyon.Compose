# Frozen traceable scoring rubric

Score complete selected-first idea, not just title. A qualification in evidence does not rescue an unconditional unsupported title. An omission is material only if it changes the selected claim, purpose, operational meaning or status. Do not require all source details or one predetermined angle.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source fidelity | Clear material invented/contradicted cause, result, status, motive or condition | Bounded but unsupported implication or material ambiguity | Every material claim source-supported or clearly qualified interpretation; conditions preserved |
| Editorial intent | Different communication task/audience or core hierarchy | Relevant but purpose/audience diluted or a secondary issue takes over | Coherent idea serves documented purpose and readers, permits multiple valid angles |
| Focus | Several incompatible stories or no proposition | Coherent topic but overbroad, checklist-like or diffuse | One developable proposition, mutually reinforcing fields |
| Distinctive angle | Generic subject interchangeable with unrelated sources | Some specifics but mostly recap | Source-specific relationship/tension with meaningful editorial insight |
| Reader appeal | Obscure, unusable or empty | Clear and relevant but flat, jargon-heavy or procedural | Clear hook and concrete value for intended readers without inflation |

Pass: valid output, fidelity2, intent2, total>=8. A semantic judge verdict is not a score. Errors are retained in denominators.

Each rating records vector, total/pass, exact output excerpt/field, exact supporting source quote or pointer, explanation of inference boundary, overclaim flags and evidence support assessment. Review C against its actual B draft and source; record corrected/introduced issues and review accuracy. For coverage use supported material claim units / inspected material claim units, allowing multiple units per field. Distinguish quote matching (mechanical) and support (editorial). Claims about audience relevance and potential reader value can be reasonable inferences; asserted audience needs/reactions require source support. A source can support an engaging interpretive headline, without proving a universal law.

No independent human or blinded scorer. The same Work assistant authors protocol/cases and performs the traceable reading. Scores are inspectable judgments, not objective labels or independently confirmed truth. Preserve borderline decisions and explain them.
