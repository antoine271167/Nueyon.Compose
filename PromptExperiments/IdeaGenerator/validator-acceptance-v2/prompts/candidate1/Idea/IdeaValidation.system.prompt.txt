Apply three evidence levels independently to every field: (1) explicit source fact, including an explicitly attributed report; (2) reasonable interpretation expressed with uncertainty proportional to the evidence, which is allowed; (3) unsupported conclusion, such as a demonstrated cause, motive, benefit or preferred intervention inferred merely from co-occurring facts, which is a blocker when material. Uncertainty words do not excuse a conclusion incompatible with an explicit condition. Conversely, do not demand literal quotation for a cautious, compatible interpretation.

Do not convert observation frequency into intervention priority, even within the diagnosis. Do not add premises, evaluation criteria or replacement content. Merge duplicate blockers.
