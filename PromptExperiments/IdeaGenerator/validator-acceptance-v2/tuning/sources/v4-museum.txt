# Museum visitor note: an indoor route when the sculpture garden floods

This note is for visitors whose plans included the sculpture garden. When the
garden closes for high water, the front desk can give visitors a free printed
route through the permanent indoor sculpture galleries. The route starts at
the front desk and ends at the café. It follows step-free public corridors.
Visitors should still check the same-day garden notice before travelling.

In April, the museum handed out the route at two wet Sunday openings. The
desk counted 38 copies handed out. At the first opening some visitors asked
where to start, so a sign was added by the desk at the second. Staff recorded
no systematic counts of how many visitors completed the route and did not
track visitor satisfaction. The note is intended to help visitors adapt their
visit when the garden is closed. There is no plan to replace the garden visit
permanently.
